## Description (paste into the channel's About)

What is under Grand Central? The Colosseum? The Burj Khalifa? Going Down takes the elevator nobody
else can: straight down through the street, past the subway lines, the forgotten tunnels and the
secret rooms, through the foundations to the bedrock, with a depth meter counting every foot and
metre. Every layer is real and sourced, drawn as a clean cutaway so you can see the whole city
stacked beneath your feet. Then we ride back up.

Three new dives a day.

Flat 2D, ink-outlined, the sample's palette: a skyline against a blue-and-dusk sky, the street, brown
soil with stones and banded grey bedrock, cut through by one big **yellow downward arrow** with a
thick ink outline. A small yellow depth meter with cream ticks on the left. No text. The arrow carries
it at 48 px.

a needle tower, an arena of arches), taxis on the street, then soil with two lit subway tunnels and
