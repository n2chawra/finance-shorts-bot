   - "Catacombs of Paris are bigger than you think": 2.4M on an 824-subscriber channel (2,900×).
   - "They Found Secret Tunnels Under the City… Then Discovered THIS": 772K on 66K.

I added this as evidence to the queued challenger `gd-hidden-hook` (first-second, metric stayed), which stays at the top of `cycle/backlog.json`. The test wording is "What's at the Bottom of <Place>?" or "What's hidden under <place>?". I didn't add a separate challenger, because two overlapping title tests would waste a cycle.
2. **Most 1M+ titles end on one emoji** (😨 😱 💀 😥). This is weak evidence, with no control group, so it stays a note and not a challenger.
