## Competitor teardown (2 channels)
**Cutaway Instinct (21.7K subs, direct competitor).** Its last 40 Shorts have **drifted away from "what's under"**. Most are now "how X is made" brand Shorts (Budweiser 499K, Bacardi 340K, Coca-Cola cans 118K) and "why this stadium does Y" (Highmark 910K, Fenway 248K, Lambeau 124K, SoFi "field buried 30 m" 70K). The typical one gets 5-50K. The one recent "under" Short ("TGL SoFi Center: What's Under the Moving Golf Green?") got 6.2K. The 60-86 s explainer format and US-arena topics did not keep its Lakers-court spike going. *For us:* the pure under-a-place dive is less contested than the 21 Nov verdict feared. Don't follow them into brands or arenas.

**From The Ground Up (81.3K subs, adjacent).** Its titles are "How a X Becomes Y" and it builds things up in flat animation. Hits: Snowdrift → Igloo 25M, Bookshelf → Hidden Room 16M, Shed → Area 51 2M, Rug → Secret Hideout 1.7M, Couch → Secret Bunker 1.4M, **Hole → Underground City 1.3M**. The common thread is a **hidden or secret space under or behind something ordinary**. Its ordinary engineering Shorts get ~30K.

## What top Shorts do that we don't
1. **They promise something hidden.** Derinkuyu 2.5M ("The underground City 😵"), Catacombs 1.8M, From The Ground Up's secret-room series. Our hook says "What's under X? Let's go down." It names the place but promises nothing.
2. **They pick places that are already famous for what's underneath** (Derinkuyu, the Catacombs, India's underwater metro). Most of our feed is famous for what's on top (Big Ben, Burj, Times Square), and the layers under those are only utilities and foundations.
3. **Craft:** Grand Central (v-20261121-53) runs 25.4 s, the top of our 20-25 s band. The contact sheet reads well: a sign and depth on every layer, and the loop matches frame 1. At 5 s the "Data:" source box sits over the top of the frame next to the meter. That is a small clutter point, noted for the reviewer, not a recipe change.
4. **Voice:** the reference Shorts are mostly music plus captions or a calm male narrator. Nothing argues against am_michael at 1.3, so no voice proposal tonight.

## Doable changes, ranked
1. **Challenger `gd-hidden-hook`** (first-second, metric stayed; at the top of `cycle/backlog.json`). The hook and title promise a hidden thing and name its kind, with no figure ("What's Hidden Under Derinkuyu?"). Evidence: From The Ground Up's secret/hidden hits (16M, 1.7M, 1.4M, 1.3M) against ~30K for its plain engineering Shorts. Effect medium, confidence medium, effort S.
2. **Shape `becomes` (trial)** in `shapes.json`: a place that was dug out over time, carved level by level on the dive. Reference: "How a Hole Becomes An Underground City", 1.3M. It fits Derinkuyu and the Catacombs.
3. **Topic sourcing** (proposal `gd-topic-mix-underneath-famous`): weight research toward places famous for what is underneath (underground cities, catacombs, cisterns, under-river metros, bunkers that are officially open) over towers whose underground is only foundations.

- Showing how to enter the closed Paris quarries. The profile forbids anything that helps someone into a restricted place.
