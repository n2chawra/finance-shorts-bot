# Going Down: quick verdict (21 Nov 2026, exploration only)

**Call: go with conditions.** The demand is real, but someone already does it.

## Scan
6 searches plus MetaBallStudios as the reference: 5,825 videos from 45 channels.
The searches were too broad ("what's under" pulled Cocomelon, toys and cartoons), so the scan's
overall medians (96K Shorts) are noise. Only the on-topic channels below count.

## Evidence
- **Cutaway Instinct**: the direct competitor. Started 11 Sep 2026; 21.6K subs and 90 Shorts in ~70 days;
  median 10.8K views. Cutaways under and inside famous places, plus how premium brands are made, 60–86 s.
  - What's Under the Lakers' Court? 1.36M (https://youtube.com/watch?v=8iiQxtSdcHw)
  - Barclays Center truck elevator and underground turntable: 1.35M (https://youtube.com/watch?v=aMAfrQR62Fw)
  - The New Highmark Stadium Has No Dome: 891K (https://youtube.com/watch?v=9sgbNVclhJ4)
- **Depth and underground demand elsewhere**
  - How deep is the ocean (Hindi): 45.6M (https://youtube.com/watch?v=N3nYajfV_Os)
  - What if your home had a secret nuclear bunker underneath? 6.2M (https://youtube.com/watch?v=nJn0SM2OjLY)

  - Dove shampoo: 11M (https://youtube.com/watch?v=ovxBG-XomKU)
  - Tea: 6.2M (https://youtube.com/watch?v=0B2y6UehUpI)

## For
- The exact idea gets 1M+ hits on a 2-month-old channel.
- Depth and "what's beneath" topics reach the tens of millions.
- Nobody does a pure 20–25 s dive with a depth meter; Cutaway is 60–86 s explainers, US arenas and brands.

## Against
- Not unique: Cutaway Instinct is 2 months ahead on almost the same idea.
- Cutaway's median is only 10.8K, so its hits are spikes, not a steady floor.
- Every dive needs true, sourced cross-sections (surveys, engineering records). Research per Short is heavy.

## Conditions
1. Make it different: 20–25 s dive, no talking-head explainer; global landmarks and cities, not US arenas.
2. Before building, watch Cutaway's top 3 to see exactly what they do.
3. If it goes ahead, re-scan with tighter searches (landmark names, "under the city", "how deep").
