# Advice (fast path, 21 Nov 2026)

The owner approved a 22 s Grand Central sample and asked to launch straight away: no full research, no further samples.
This file records what the build rests on.

**Make:** one place per Short, 20–25 s, three a day.
- **Landmark dive** (core): about half of all Shorts.
- **Station dive** (core): about a third.
- **Foundation dive** (core): the rest.
- At least a third of places are outside the US and UK.

**Evidence** (scan, 21 Nov):
- Cutaway Instinct, which started 11 Sep 2026, has a median of 10,776 views per Short. Two hits sit far above that:
  "What's Under the Lakers' Court?" (1,357,868) and Barclays Center's underground turntable (1,348,561).
- Depth and underground Shorts can travel very far: a secret bunker under a house got 6,183,044 and "How deep is the
  ocean" got 45,635,059.
- The 20–35 s band had the best median in the scan (184,772). The scan was noisy, so read that as a direction only.

**Avoid:** "how it's made" brand Shorts, which are the other half of Cutaway Instinct's channel. Avoid places whose
layers can't be sourced.

**Look:** the approved sample (`remotion/src/dive/README.md`, kit) is the reference.

**First second:** the landmark is named on screen and in the first spoken sentence, which asks what is under it. The
first dive starts by 2.5 s. The answer (the deepest figure) stays out of the title.

**What this build skipped:** reference shot sheets, topic-family labels and comments. The weekly loop's challengers
(`cycle/backlog.json`) test the open questions instead.
