# Quick verdict: India maps (Hindi)

**Call: go, with conditions.** India-map Shorts get hundreds of thousands of views, and small channels get found:
every small channel in the scan had a Short at 5x its own median. The hits are flat map animations our kit can
draw. Indian views pay very little, so this channel lives on Shorts volume. It has to stay on India's official
map and away from the communal framing many niche hits use.

Scan of 24 Nov 2026 (`scan.json`): 6 India searches (English, Hinglish, Hindi; region IN) and 3 references
(Mihul Rawat, Ankit map, Gyano Trick) found videos from 45 channels. The searches also caught news (India Today,
Times Now), exam prep (StudyIQ, Drishti, UPSC Wallah) and The Lallantop, so the numbers below use the
**26 on-niche map and geography channels**.

## Money

- **Path:** 10M Shorts views in 90 days (about 110K a day). At the on-niche median that needs one breakout or
  100K+ medians, which the top map channels reach. 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours is slower without long videos.
- **RPM:** Indian Hindi Shorts earn very little, an estimated $0.01–0.03 per 1,000 views; Hindi long videos about
  $0.3–1 (creator reports; estimates that vary widely). Advertiser-friendly as long as it stays off communal topics.
- **Other income:** little at first; later, exam-prep and edtech sponsors, who already buy this audience.

| USD a month (estimates) | Low | Likely | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Month 6 | 0 | 20 | 200 |
| Month 12 | 5 | 80 | 600 |

Assumptions: 2–3 Shorts a day; partner programme around month 3–6 if a Short breaks out; the high case needs
100K+ medians and a few long videos from month 7. This is the platform's lowest-paying channel per view; it earns
on volume.

## Can we make it

- **Look: yes, with one build.** The hits are flat maps filling in state by state, icons, flags and small figures:
  no footage, no faces. Our flat kit and directed shots already draw better than most of these one-person channels.
  The build: an India layer on **India's official boundary** (all of J&K, Ladakh, Arunachal) with state and
  district shapes from an openly licensed source (DataMeet or Survey of India; licence confirmed during the build).
  Natural Earth's India must not be used.
- **Voice: yes, with checks.** Kokoro's Hindi voices (hf_alpha, hf_beta, hm_omega, hm_psi) are its weaker ones;
  the owner picks one by ear at 1.5×. Numbers spoken as Hindi words and Devanagari captions are build items
  (Poppins already has Devanagari glyphs).
- **Policy: care.** A wrong map of India is illegal there. Many niche hits ride religion ("which god in which
  state"), "Akhand Bharat" or "invaders changed India's map": not ours. Partition, divisions and borders only as
  sourced history. Drawn maps need no AI label. Not made for kids.

## Conditions

1. Official-boundary India map layer before the first sample.
2. Hindi voice, Hindi number words and Devanagari captions checked on sample 1.
3. Profile rules: no religion-vs-religion, Kashmir opinions, party politics or communal framing.
4. Shorts first, 2–3 a day, 20–50 s; long videos only after a Short passes 500K.
5. Full research uses the 26 on-niche channels, not the news and exam channels.
