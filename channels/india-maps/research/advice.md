## Kinds to avoid

- **Religion and communal framing**: 73 Shorts, median 107,137. They get views, but they break the profile:
  gods by state, "Akhand Bharat", "invaders changed India's map".
- **Politics** (parties, leaders, caste of chief ministers): 11 Shorts, median 139,715. Same reason.
- **Cricket and celebrities** on a map (21, median 158,772): the map is decoration, the topic isn't ours, and real
  people's photos are hard to license.
- **Map basics** ("India political map, all states"): 27, median 8,578, tier avoid.
- **Projects and rivers as Shorts**: 43, median 23,609, tier avoid (better as long videos later).

## How many to post

`counts.json`: **1 Short a day**, 0 long. Channels with a hit post about 0.7 a day; at 1 a day each weekly-loop test
takes about 16 days. The owner's notes said 20–40 s Shorts and implied more; **2 a day** halves the loop time and
fits the quota (9 of 100 uploads used today). Your call on the Launch review.

## Shorts, long or both

**Shorts only at launch.** India long videos: median 35,632 but falling (0.66), only 1 of 3 small channels had a long
hit. Revisit when a Short passes 500K (long candidates: rivers and dams, state histories, 10–15 min band best at 42K).

## The look

The niche is **maps on a relief base**: 15 of 21 sampled shots are maps; no presenters, almost no characters.
- A shaded-relief South Asia base (Natural Earth raster, public domain; no clouds), India on top as a flat painted
  shape with thin state lines, on **India's official boundary** (new India layer; never Natural Earth's India).
- Every sentence changes the map: paint a state in, split a region with a drawn line, push in and pull back, a big
  year label, a flag sticker, die-cut stickers of things (Commons photos) dropping onto states.
- The every-state format fills the map state by state and ends on the fully coloured map (the loop).
- Devanagari labels and chapter titles; word-by-word Devanagari captions from the Kokoro timings.
- Nothing in `notOurs`: no footage or faces to replace.

## The first second

India already on screen at frame 0 with the subject state lit; the state named by 1 s; a question in the first line
(3 of 4 references open on one) or in the title; a figure, when the story has one, by 3 s on a label on the map.

## Sound

Dark, low cinematic beds about 25 dB under the voice; a whoosh on every camera move and paint-in, a low hit on the key
date and the payoff, about 12 effects a minute; a short drop before the payoff (`research/sound.md`).

## What the verdict got wrong

- It called "how a state was divided" the lead format, after the 24 Nov notes; the labels put **every-state map fills**
  first (350 Shorts, 26% hits) and formation third.
- The family medians are far above the verdict's on-niche median (25,365) because that median includes the exam-prep and
  GK-trick uploads of a few map channels; the earnings estimates stay as they are.
- No comments were collected by the scan, so viewers' own topic asks are not in this research.
