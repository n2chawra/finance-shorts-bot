# India Maps

**Who it's for.** Hindi speakers in India, mostly 15–35, on phones, who like knowing their own state and
country better: students, people from small towns and big cities, families who share "look, our state". They search
in Roman script ("har state ka", "india map") and comment in Hinglish ("mera state?"). They know India's map from school
and want the surprising thing on it.

**What it covers.** Animated India maps telling one true thing at a time:
- every state's ___ on one map (official animal, bird, tree, capital, biggest city, year it was formed, language,
  number-plate code…), the map filling state by state;
- how a state or UT was formed, split, merged or joined India, as dated history;
- India against the world (borders, size, a straight line from an Indian city);
- a state against a country (people, area), the figures computed by code from sourced data.

**What it never covers.** Religion against religion, gods or temples ranked by state, caste, "invaders" framing,
"Akhand Bharat", political parties or leaders, Kashmir opinions, anything that mocks a state, a community or a
neighbour, news commentary, cricket and film stars. No "hidden truth" without a source.

**Where facts come from.** Government of India (Census of India, Ministry of Home Affairs, India Code, PIB, Know
India, Survey of India), state government sites, the Election Commission, World Bank and UN for other countries,
Britannica, and Wikipedia only where it cites a source. Every figure on screen is a sourced fact or computed by code
from one; the source line names it.

**The rules of this field.**
- **India's official map, always.** The whole of Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh (including the areas held by Pakistan
  and China) and Arunachal Pradesh are drawn as India, as the Survey of India map shows. The only India outline is
  `remotion/src/flat/india.tsx`; never another map's outline, never a dotted "disputed" line inside India.
- Neighbours are named respectfully and drawn as they are; no maps that show India's land as theirs.
- Partition, divisions and wars only as dated, sourced history: what happened, when, where the line went. No blame,
  no casualty counts as a hook, no sides.
- State pride without ranking states as better or worse people; a "most/least" list only on a measured figure.
- Hindi narration in everyday spoken Hindi (not shuddh Hindi): common English words (बॉर्डर, स्टेट, मैप) written in
  Devanagari. Titles in English or Roman-script Hinglish.
- The disclaimer (channel.json) goes in every description.

**Risks turned into rules.** A wrong map of India is illegal there and costs the channel: QA fails any frame with
another India outline. Communal topics get views in this niche but are not ours. Indian views pay little per view,
so the channel lives on Shorts that people share and comment on.
