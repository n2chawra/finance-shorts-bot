# Branding kit: India Maps (Hindi)

Made 24 Nov 2026. Create the channel on YouTube with one of these (a Brand Account on The Why Atlas Gmail), then
click **Connect YouTube** on the Channels page.

## Name ideas (handles checked on YouTube on 24 Nov 2026)

| Name | Handle | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Naksha Bharat** (pick) | @NakshaBharat (free) | "naksha" is the word Hindi viewers use for a map, "Bharat" says whose; short, easy to say aloud, works in Devanagari (नक्शा भारत) and Roman |
| **Har State Ka** | @HarStateKa (free) | the core format's own words ("every state's ___"), the phrase people type; narrower if the channel grows past every-state videos |
| **Bharat Ka Naksha** | @BharatKaNaksha (free) | a full Hindi phrase, very clear, a little long for a channel name |

Taken: @NakshaKahani ("Naksha Kahani"), @StateWiseIndia ("Bharat Explorer").

## Description (paste into the channel's About)

भारत का नक्शा, हर दिन एक नई कहानी के साथ। कौन-सा राज्य कब बना, हर स्टेट का ऑफिशियल जानवर, पक्षी या भाषा क्या है,
और भारत दुनिया के सामने कहाँ खड़ा है: सब कुछ एनिमेटेड नक्शों पर, सच्चे और सोर्स किए गए तथ्यों के साथ।
नक्शे भारत के आधिकारिक नक्शे के अनुसार।

India's map, one story a day: when each state was formed, every state's official animal, bird or language, and where
India stands against the world. Animated maps, true and sourced facts, India's official map.
