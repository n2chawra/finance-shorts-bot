## Policy and algorithm (tonight's topic)
- **Nothing new in November.** The newest changes are older:
  - **July:** YouTube clarified its "inauthentic content" rule. Generic or repetitive videos, unsatisfying ones, and AI
    personas on sensitive topics are not monetised. Using AI is not the trigger.
  - **October:** Shorts that re-upload other people's clips lose reach.
  - We are on the right side of both, because every Short is a new script, a new map and a new story.
    `generator/variety.py` is the defence, and it only gets stricter.
- **The 2027 monetisation bar is now confirmed by several outlets** (one source on 21 Nov):
  - From 1 Feb 2027, a new Partner Program channel needs 8,000 watch hours in 12 months or 20M Shorts views in 90 days.
    Today the bar is 4,000 hours or 10M views.
  - This channel has about 17K views in total, so it will have to reach the new bar.
  - Routed `owner` as an `info` notification. Nothing in how we make videos changes.
