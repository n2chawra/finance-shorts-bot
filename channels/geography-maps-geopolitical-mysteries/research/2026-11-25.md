## Learn from our own numbers
- Our two best by avg viewed are why_shaped stories with a "Why…" question title (83%, 74.5%).
- The three worst are two what-ifs (Suez, Sahara) and one why_shaped (English cities).
  - The worst what-if titles state a scenario ("What if the Suez Canal closed for a year?"); the best name a strange thing you can see ("hide a power station inside a mountain").
  - That supports the existing challenger `geo-title-names-the-oddity`, so I'm proposing no new test.
- Why I still added three what-ifs: the feed had 1 open what_if_world topic against 15 why_shaped. The planner can't tell the same format twice in a row, so the next video run would have stalled for a what-if.
