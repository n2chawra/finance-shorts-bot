## Titles: what the niche's numbers say
I compared 2,891 English-titled Shorts in the scan (outlier = views ÷ the channel's subscribers):

| Title kind | Shorts | Median outlier | Hit rate (≥3×) | Median views |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What if… | 117 | 1.52 | 23% | 32.8K |
| Question (why/how/can…, or ends in "?") | 991 | 1.06 | 19% | 14.7K |
| Statement | 1,783 | 0.98 | 16% | 19.2K |

Emoji, hashtags and digits in a title make no difference (1.07 vs 0.98, 1.01 vs 1.00, 1.01 vs 1.00).

**What this means for us:** our title rule (a question naming the place, and "What if" for what-ifs) matches what wins in this niche, so no title challenger tonight. The biggest small-channel hits name a well-known place and ask why: "Why Chile Is So Long & Narrow", "Why Denmark Owns Greenland" (6.0M), "Why People Settled the Most Remote Island" (3.5M), "Why Cyprus Is Divided" (5.0M).
