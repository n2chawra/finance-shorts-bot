  - If it loses, the recipe's pace line should say what the champion actually speaks.
- **The music sits low.** At about 31 dB under the voice the bed is close to inaudible on a phone.

  - Queued last among the look tests as **`geo-music-closer`** (about 20 dB under).
- **Effects:** ours sit inside the measured 3–5 a minute. No change.
- **Voice character:** a calm British explainer matches the references' tone, so I'm proposing no change of narrator.

**Facts**
- **Dingo Fence:**
  - 5,614 km long; once over 8,000 km.
  - Building began in 1880.
  - 180 cm mesh.
  - About 20,000 sheep were lost a year before its gaps were sealed.
- **Canada's lakes:**
  - 879,800 lakes of 10 ha or more; Russia has 201,200, out of 1.42M in the world (McGill, Nature Communications 2016). Code computes Canada's share (about 62%).
  - 563 lakes over 100 km² (ECCC).

**The `verify` lists flag what the fact reviewer must check**
- Dingo Fence:
  - The researcher's claim about keeping First Nations people off the land must be attributed or left out.
  - Any distance comparison must be computed from sourced distances.
- Canada:
  - Lake counts differ between studies, so the script says "about" and names the study.
  - Never "more lakes than the rest of the world combined" (Snopes rates it false).
  - The glacial origin needs an official source.
