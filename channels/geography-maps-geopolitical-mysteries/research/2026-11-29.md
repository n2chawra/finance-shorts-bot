- **What it does:**
  - **The whole world changes by one rule**, stated in a "What If Every…" title.
  - **It mixes in a game.** GTA and Minecraft hooks pull in viewers who aren't geography fans.
  - **About half its hits are pure fiction with no figures**, such as borders moving at midnight or a reality-show vote.
- **What we take:** the rule-for-the-whole-world framing is already our `the-recolour` shape. The one real-world rule here
  that nobody has told this month is the biome map, so that became tonight's topic.
- **What we leave:**
  - Its fiction, because our pack says true stories.
  - "Steal back what was taken" (Crimea, Kashmir and the like), which takes sides on disputed land.
