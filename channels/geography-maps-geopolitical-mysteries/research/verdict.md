# Quick verdict: Geography, maps & geopolitical mysteries

**Go, with conditions.** Strong Shorts demand that small channels break into, and animated maps suit
our flat kit. Long videos only win for big channels.

Scan of 17 Nov 2026 (`scan.json`): 45 channels found by 6 searches and the 3 references, their last 6
months of uploads, 3,600 videos, 1,397 quota units. The searches also pulled in off-niche channels
(gangster stories, animal facts, exam prep); the numbers below count only on-niche titles (maps,
countries, borders, "what if", regions).

## Money (estimates, not sourced figures)

- Partner programme: the Shorts path (10M views in 90 days) fits this niche's numbers if one Short
  breaks out; likely month 4–8.
- Advertisers: geography and maps are advertiser-friendly; wars and disputed borders can lose ads.
- Shorts RPM about $0.03–0.06 per 1,000 views (education/geography band).
- Earnings a month: 6 months $0–400 (likely ~$25); 12 months $10–2,000 (likely ~$150). Assumes 2
  Shorts a day and no long videos in the first 6 months.

## Doable by us

- **Look:** yes, with one new component: country outlines from Natural Earth (public domain), drawn
  and animated on the flat kit. The hits show maps, flags and simple characters; no footage or faces.
- **Voice:** English, a Kokoro US or UK narrator.
- **Policy:** disputed borders and wars need care. A map of India that doesn't follow the official
  borders can break Indian law; any disputed border follows the official map of the country the
  video is about, or is left out. No AI-content label for maps and drawings.

## Conditions

1. The map component is built before the samples.
2. Disputed borders follow the official map of the country a video is about, or are left out; every
   fact is sourced.
3. Shorts only at launch; long videos once a Short passes 100K views.
4. The full research drops the off-niche channels before ranking topic families.
