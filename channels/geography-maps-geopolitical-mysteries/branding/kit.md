Made 17 Nov 2026. Create the channel on YouTube with one of these, then click **Connect YouTube** on the
Channels page.

## Description (paste into the channel's About)

Why is Chile so long? Why does Gambia sit inside Senegal? What if the whole world became one country?
Short, true answers to the strangest questions on the map: borders, shapes, rivers, seas and the
"what ifs" that would change everything. A new map mystery every day.

Maps are simplified; borders are shown as recognised by the country each video is about. Every fact is
sourced.

## Keywords

geography, maps, borders, countries, what if, geopolitics, world map, map facts, why countries are
shaped, strange borders, enclaves, landlocked countries, time zones, canals, straits
