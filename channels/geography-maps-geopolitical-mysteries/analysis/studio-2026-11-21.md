## What stands out

1. **Stayed to watch is 60–64% on 5 of 6 Shorts.** Paisa Panchayat swiped away 55–76% in its first month (24–45% stayed).
2. **4 of 6 Shorts got 700–1,200 views within 48 hours.** Paisa Panchayat never passed ~1,200 on any Short in a month.
3. **The feed pushes them:** 96% of views come from the Shorts feed, almost none from subscribers (0.1%), so the topic and the first second earn the reach, not the audience.
4. **An older, US audience on TVs:** 47.6% US; 35–44 is the top age band and 33% are 45+; 39% of watch time is on a TV.
5. **Length doesn't hurt:** every Short is 61–71 s, and Greenland held 75% of a 63 s Short (47 s on average).
6. **Weak spots:**
   - Dinorwig: 104 views despite 64% stayed. The feed didn't test it widely; the topic is niche (one Welsh power station).
   - Tristan da Cunha: 43.6% stayed, the lowest. It opens on a "who would choose to live" question rather than a "why" fact.
   - Suez: 12 views. It was the first upload, before the channel had any history.
7. **Title pattern of the winners:** a familiar place plus "Why?" or a big what-if ("Greenland belongs to Denmark. Why?", "The Sahara used to be green. Why?", "One passport for every UN country?").
8. **Bug:** every description carries "Illustrative only. Not financial advice." and "The characters are not advisers.", which is Paisa Panchayat's disclaimer on a geography channel.
