## Description (paste into the channel's About)

What actually happens when you tap send? How does a chip smaller than a fingernail hold billions of
switches? How does an AI pick its next word, and why does your phone get hot? We open up the things you
use every day (chips, AI, phones, the internet, gadgets) and show how they really work, part by part, in
3D. Short answers most days, the full story in longer videos.

Simplified for clarity; every fact and figure is sourced.
