## What top Shorts do that we don't
1. **Their titles state, ours ask.** 16 of the 19 references at 500K+ views use a statement that names the thing and
   promises how it works ("How Electrical Outlets Work" 77.3M, "How a Dam Generates Electricity" 8.7M, "How Starlink
   Actually Works" 4.6M, "How GPUs Are Printed With Light" 1.0M). Only 3 ask a question, and their median is 2.1M
   against 4.6M for the statements. The week's biggest on-profile breakout is just "NVIDIA's First Chip" (1.78M on
   29.8K subscribers). All 8 of our titles are questions ("Why Does Hoover Dam Need Lake Mead to Be Full?"). That
   comes from the shared planner rule, which was drawn from money Shorts (13 of 20 asked a question), not from this
   niche. The statements still hold back the answer, so they keep to our lesson that the title must not give away
   the payoff. This is now a first-second challenger: `tech-title-how-it-works`.
2. **Descriptions**: ours open by repeating the title as a question, then say what we build in 3D, then the sources
   and the disclaimer. The references' descriptions are mostly hashtags. No evidence that this matters for
   retention, so no proposal.
3. **The first frame**: the open challengers (`tech-mechanism-by-6s`, `tech-figure-first`, `tech-claim-hook`) already
   cover it. Nothing new tonight.
