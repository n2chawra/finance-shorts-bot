## What top Shorts do that we don't
1. **One repeatable shape, named in the title** (You Built A...): the viewer knows the whole promise from the title
   (a small thing becomes a giant one) and every step is a real machine at a bigger scale. We have "build it up" as a
   device, but no story shape that climbs from the viewer's hand to an industrial machine. Added as a trial shape
   (`small-to-giant`) and a first-second challenger (`tech-small-to-giant`, last in its kind: one channel, low
   confidence).
2. **A myth corrected with a picture** (3D Eye's airplane): the subject everyone has used, the mechanism shown in
   3D. The common school explanation of lift is wrong (NASA Glenn), which suits our audience of experts who correct
   bad explanations. Turned into tonight's topic.
3. **"| 3D Animation" in the title** (3D Eye, Engineer Explain, Professor Of AI's "(3D)"): checked in the scan, no
   signal. Shorts from the last 60 days with "3D" in the title have a median 0.94x their channel's median views
   (n=321) against 1.00x without (n=1,634); inside channels that use both it goes either way (Engineering School
   and Professor Of AI higher, Dr Pradeep Havagond and Medical Animation Media lower). Not proposed.
4. Voice: not readable from the pages tonight; nothing new against the 18-21 Nov notes (single calm narrators).
