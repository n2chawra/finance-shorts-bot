## What top Shorts do that we don't (titles, tonight's topic)
These are titles of tech Shorts from the scan, each compared with its own channel's median (n = 3,594):

| Title shape | n | median vs channel | hit rate | vs the rest |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| contains "invent" | 34 | **2.29×** | **53%** | 1.0×, 30% |
| numbered series ("Part 4") | 25 | **1.46×** | 44% | 1.0×, 30% |
| says "you" / "your" | 426 | **0.81×** | **20%** | 1.02×, 31% |
| "What happens / what if" | 113 | 0.82× | 26% | 1.0×, 30% |
| "How … works" | 391 | 0.89× | 29% | 1.0×, 30% |
| question mark, digit, emoji, hashtags, "(3D Animation)", length ≤ 7 words | — | 0.98–1.07× | 28–33% | no effect |

- **"You/your" titles do worse**, and the gap holds inside channels: lower in 19 of the 29 channels that
  use both kinds (Inside Decoded, the closest to our look: 0.46× against 1.71× over 31 titles; Business
  Insider 0.42× vs 1.12×). Three of our open titles say "Your" (face unlock, keyboard, message). This goes on
  the challenger list (`tech-title-no-you`), not straight into the recipe.
- **Invention and series titles do better, but the effect is mostly one channel.** Logibu's "Most Important
  Invention In History, Part N" ran 23.9× its own median (5.35M and 3.96M views on 36K subscribers). Outside
  Logibu, "invent" is mixed (Engineer Explain 2.2×, AI Tech 3.7 0.68×). Series titles were higher in 4 of 5
  channels, so they go on the list as a low-confidence challenger (`tech-title-series`). Invention topics
  were already a core family. Tonight's new topics lean on it.
- **Hashtags, emoji and "(3D Animation)" in the title make no difference.** Keep descriptions and titles
  clean. Nothing to change.
- **First frame**: `style.json` already measured it on 19 references (the subject is on screen by 2 s in
  84%, a figure by 3 s in 11%), and the recipe follows it. There is nothing new until we have our own previews.

**Breakouts in the scan** (views ≥ 3× the channel's subscribers, on our subjects): a dam explainer
