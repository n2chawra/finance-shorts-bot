# Quick verdict: How tech and AI actually work

**Go, with conditions.** Long explainers are where this niche pays, and small channels break in there. The biggest Shorts are 3D, and we build the 3D engine for it.

Scan: 6 searches + 3 references → 45 channels, 3,924 videos (`scan.json`, 18 Nov). About 30 of the 45 channels were off-niche (3D "what's inside" cutaways, anatomy, Hindi engineering), so the numbers below count **tech-titled videos only** (328 Shorts, 85 long).

## For
- **Small channels win long.** 4 of 8 tech channels under 100K subs had a long video at 5× their median. Silicon Up Close (12.7K subs): "What Happens Inside an AI Chip When You Ask a Question" 1.02M ([oBKrR3i6jWM](https://youtube.com/watch?v=oBKrR3i6jWM)), a CPU in one second 447K ([r9qDcQ_b50k](https://youtube.com/watch?v=r9qDcQ_b50k)), phone cameras 270K ([5DmLxVhCYBI](https://youtube.com/watch?v=5DmLxVhCYBI)). Xplained with Mushrooms (2.3K subs): 622K ([vIM9qCyfVmg](https://youtube.com/watch?v=vIM9qCyfVmg)).
- **Long demand:** tech long videos median 23.5K views in 90 days, 21% hits; 600–900 s band 19% hits.
- **Evergreen:** Starlink 4.6M ([DW-aL_MiUz4](https://youtube.com/watch?v=DW-aL_MiUz4)), sea-cable maker 5.5M ([cKnwkxItL2M](https://youtube.com/watch?v=cKnwkxItL2M)), both months old and still the top.

- **The biggest Shorts are 3D:** Professor Of AI's 3D AI skits 10–18M each on 400K subs (e.g. [-8XlWwznPHE](https://youtube.com/watch?v=-8XlWwznPHE)); Branch Education's 3D cutaways. We build this look.

## Against
- **Shorts are thin:** median 4.0K views in 90 days (267 Shorts) vs 20K for geography. Small channels' Short hits reach 50–160K (Inside Decoded, [WIF1fFTU4LI](https://youtube.com/watch?v=WIF1fFTU4LI) 161K).
- **Build cost:** the 3D engine is new work (1–2 weeks) before sample 1; 3D renders are slower.
- **Crowded at the top:** ColdFusion 1.2M, Branch Education 770K, Asianometry 230K median per long video.

## Conditions
1. Long-first: 1 long a week, 12–16 min, "what happens inside X when you Y"; 2 Shorts a day.
2. Build a **real 3D engine** (Remotion + three.js) before sample 1: cutaways, exploded parts, zooms through scales (phone → chip → transistor), glowing signal paths, a stylised 3D character for skit Shorts. About 1–2 weeks.
3. Every claim sourced; no rumours, no AI hype as fact, no reviews.
4. Full research measures only on-niche videos.
