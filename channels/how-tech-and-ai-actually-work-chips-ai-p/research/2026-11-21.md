## Policy: what changed and what it means for us
1. **Partner Program overhaul, effective 1 Feb 2027** (announced 10 Aug 2026). New applicants: 1,000 subscribers
   plus **8,000** watch hours in 365 days (now 4,000) or **20M** Shorts views in 90 days (now 10M). The Shorts
   revenue pool also needs 10M qualified Shorts views every 90 days; an activity rule (1,000 hours, 1M Shorts
   views, or 2 long videos / 5 Shorts per 90 days) applies to every Partner. We cannot reach the old bar by
   31 Jan, so for this long-first channel the realistic route is watch hours: 8,000 hours is about 40,000 full
   views of a 12-minute video. Routed `owner` (info).
2. **AI labels**: disclosure is required for photorealistic AI-made or altered content; since 27 May 2026 YouTube
   labels undisclosed photorealistic AI itself. Our videos are clearly animated illustrations with a synthetic
   narrator, not realistic people or events: no change. Keep the "illustration / not to scale" tags the profile asks for.
3. **Inauthentic content** (renamed July 2025, clarified July 2026; a January 2026 wave removed mass-produced AI
   channels): template-based, repetitive videos, made with AI or not, lose monetisation channel-wide. Our defence is
   what we already do: a different directed picture, place and story every video (`generator/variety.py`), real
   narration with depth, sourced facts. With five channels on one engine, the risk to watch is look sameness across
   a channel's Shorts; the auditor's picture tally is the right place to catch it. No change proposed tonight.
4. Nothing new for Shorts length, ranking or the Shorts feed was announced in Oct-Nov 2026 in what I read.
