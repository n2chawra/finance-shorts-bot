## What top Shorts do that we don't
1. **Denser machine sound.** The two biggest outliers among AI-voiced 3D Shorts run far more effects than we allow:
   AI Tech 3.7's dam Short (8.6M views on 11K subscribers) about 20 a minute, Inside Decoded 30-32 a minute.
   Branch Education sits at 8-12, Professor Of AI about 10. Our cap is 12 a minute (`channel.json` sound), mixed at
   -29 to -31 dB mean, 12 dB under the voice. Counts were taken by eye and the dam sheet's sound probe was cut
   short, so this is a low-confidence look test: `tech-sfx-dense`, measured on held after the hook.
2. **Pace.** After the owner's note the narrator went from 0.92 to 1.0 ("about 164 words a minute"), but the three
   Shorts since measure 140-148 words a minute (words ÷ duration in `timeline.json`; the 0.92 microwave was 130).
   The gap looks like the padding between beats, not the voice. 5 of 16 references are "fast" narrators. Proposal
   `tech-wpm-gap` for the improver to check (and close the owner's note properly) before any pace test.
3. **Music and voice level: nothing to change.** Ours sits 26 dB under the voice (references measured ~24 dB on
   18 Nov), low dark beds as the references; one calm male narrator (`am_michael`) as Branch and ColdFusion.
4. **Retention update (not sound):** the transistor curve now shows its biggest drop, 24.5 points, at 6.2 s on
   "The transistor. What did the very first one look like?", a re-asked question with nothing moving. All three
   curves lose most at 6-8 s on a line that isn't the mechanism; added to `tech-mechanism-by-6s`'s evidence.
