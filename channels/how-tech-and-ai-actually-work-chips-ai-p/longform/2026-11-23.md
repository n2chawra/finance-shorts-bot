## Craft findings (script structure)
- **Walk the object.** The Crossrail Short orders its beats along the machine's physical path and opens each with the
  viewer's question ("How?", "Why doesn't the ground cave in?", "Where does it go?"). That is a ready-made chapter
  spine for 7-8 minutes: one part per chapter, the causal step on its own, the hardest moment last.
- **One moment of one action.** Long titles framed on a single moment ("in one second", "when you ...") hit 3x their
  channel's median 2 of 4 times (median 10x) vs 13% for the rest (n = 215). Tiny sample; an experiment arm, not a rule.
- **No long format for machines.** Four 1M+ videos (BuildWitt tunnelling 3.0M, Starship's 33 engines 2.1M, WELT tunnel
  machines 1.8M, Starfactory 1.2M) share a "how the machine works" shape our long formats lack; the rocket and Crossrail
  topics are filed under `inside_when_you` for now.
