# Niche-scan backtest on Paisa Panchayat (2026-11-17)

The multi-channel plan's check: scan our own niche as if the channel were new, and see whether the
public numbers already show what the channel learned in its first month. Scan: `scan.json`
(45 channels, 4,008 scored videos, 1,382 quota units). Families labelled by keyword rules (874 of
4,015 titles), ranked by `niche-scan families`.

| Lesson channel #1 learned | Scan says | Found? |
|---|---|---|
| Billionaire and money-at-scale stories beat personal budget | billionaires core 1.17× (20% hits); personal budget tips last, 0.89× (7% hits) | **Yes** |
| News doesn't work | "market news" came out core, 1.26× (27% hits, 90 Shorts) | **No** |
| Shorts of 35 s or less beat longer ones | every length band sits at 0.95–1.08× its channel's median | **No** (not visible in public data) |
| A figure in the title helps | figure 1.08× and 18% hits vs 1.00× and 14% | Weakly |

Reading: the scan finds the topic ranking that mattered most (big money over budget tips), but not
the length lesson or the news lesson. Length probably needs the channel's own retention, which only
comes after launch (the weekly loop's job). The news result may be the keyword labels ("today" and
"crash" catch more than news explainers); the real research labels every title with Claude and should
be re-checked against this table.
