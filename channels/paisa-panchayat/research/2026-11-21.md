## YouTube Shorts policy and algorithm (tonight's topic)
1. **No Shorts policy change in November.** The latest changes are below.
2. **Partner Program, from 1 Feb 2027** (already in our notifications since 10 Aug):
   - New entry bar: 8,000 watch hours in 365 days, or 20M Shorts views in 90 days.
   - **Shorts ads go only to channels with 10M Shorts views in the last 90 days**, however they joined.
   - Fan funding is unchanged: 500 subscribers plus 3,000 long-form hours or 3M Shorts views.

   For a 14-subscriber channel, long-video watch hours remain the realistic path to revenue. The owner notification stays as written.
3. **Originality update, 1 Oct 2026** (TeamYouTube Help Community post; Rene Ritchie on Creator Insider):
   - Shorts recommendations now favour original clips and cut reach for channels that re-upload others' videos.
   - Per Ritchie, these do **not** count as original: voice-over that only describes what's on screen, minor technical edits, and "template-based bulk changes".

   We're on the right side of this: our own scripts, our own flat-kit animation and our own synthesized audio. The phrase "template-based" is the reminder: the variety rules and the directed-shot rule (a place built for each line) are what keep us original. Nothing to change.
4. **Inauthentic content, clarified 16 July:** three kinds lose monetization:
   - near-identical templated videos
   - emotionally manipulative formulas
   - **AI personas presenting finance, health or legal information as experts**

   I checked us against this. Our narrator is an unnamed voice and the cast are cartoon characters, and nobody is presented as a financial expert or adviser. The description disclaimer ("Illustrative only. Not financial advice.") fits. Rule to keep: never brand a cast member or the narrator as an adviser.
5. **Ranking:**
   - No primary source confirms a "swiped away" ranking rule, or any length or completion threshold. Claims like "under 30 s with 85% completion gets a boost" are SEO-blog assertions.
   - YouTube defines an engaged view as watching past the first seconds, the same thing our evaluator's "stayed to watch" measures.
   - Search Engine Journal reports, unconfirmed, that Shorts older than 30 days get fewer views. That fits our habit of riding a moment (GTA VI, Cyber Monday) while it's live.
