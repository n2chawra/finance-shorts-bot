## Craft findings (script structure)
- **The README still describes the old 5-9 minute video.** "Pace and length" asks for 6-9 chapters and a recap every 2-3 minutes, and the story spine asks for "4 or more" levels. The length is 180-240 s and scout.md says 3-4 items. L-20261111-01 followed the README: 6 chapters, 63 beats, 380 s. At 3-4 minutes there is room for 3-4 chapters of about 50 s and exactly one recap.
- **Six identical chapters.** Every chapter of L-20261111-01 ran the same 8 beats: question, figure, comparison, aside, tease. The cards were labels ("Way one. Spend it."), not questions.
- **No loop spans the video.** The hook answered the title ("This is ... Now picture one warehouse room"). Every tease closed within 60 s, so after the hook nothing was held back for the end.
- **Our long videos come with their questions built in.** The viewer who clicks has just watched the Short, and each topic's angle already lists the 3-4 questions the Short left open. Those questions are the chapter list.
