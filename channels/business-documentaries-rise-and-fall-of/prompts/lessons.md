# Lessons from our own videos

Each lesson is a mistake that cost viewers, written by the reviewer (agents/reviewer.md) from where viewers
left. The writer follows them and QA enforces them. A new channel starts with channel #1's general ones.

## 1. The second beat stays on the thing from frame 1 and adds a surprise (reviewer, 2026-11-25; raised 2026-11-28)
Seen in: every Short with drop points loses the most at its second beat, and the more beat 2 leaves the hook's thing,
the steeper the fall. v-20261124-32 Marvel, −34.4% at 5.6 s, the channel's worst drop: Captain America left the screen
for a 1997 10-K page of small type while the line went back in time ("The decade before, Marvel had gone bankrupt").
v-20261122-33 Tamagotchi, −31.2% at 5.4 s: Bandai's glass head office and a launch date ("Bandai put it on sale in Japan
in November 1996"). v-20261125-32 Blockbuster, −20.7% at 6.3 s: the store and the envelope left for black-and-white
polaroids of Netflix's two founders and "So what happened?"; stayed 41.9%, 13 points under the channel's 54.8% median.
v-20261123-32 Spanx, −19.2% at 6.9 s: Sara Blakely left for a black-and-white stock photo of legs in pantyhose.
v-20261120-09 Stanley, −19.0% at 5.5 s: a black-and-white museum case of old flasks and a walk-back ("it barely
sold"); stayed 29.1%. v-20261124-31 Juicero, −18.8% at 8.2 s: one stock photo of chopped vegetables held about 6 s.
The one that kept its subject lost least: v-20261123-31 Krispy Kreme, −12.8% at 7.8 s, the Krispy Kreme truck with a
BROKE UP stamp; it has the channel's best stayed (57.4%) and held after hook (37.2%).
Fix: beat 2 (about 3.5–9 s) keeps the hook's object or person on screen (the toy, the cup, the hero, the store) and
its line adds the next surprise about it (a figure, a turn). No backstory in beat 2: no "the decade before", no
launch date, no maker or head office, no "not new / barely sold" walk-back, no "So what happened?". Director: beat 2's
main layer is never a document page of small type, a building, a black-and-white or archive photo, or a generic stock
picture; a document may come in as an overlay on the hook's thing.

## 2. The first frame names the subject (general)
Seen in: channel #1's first month, 55–76% swiped away when the title and first second held the subject back.
Fix: the title and the first spoken sentence name who or what the video is about.

## 3. Only what is true (general)
Seen in: channel #1, invented examples lost to real, named stories.
Fix: a true channel tells sourced facts about real, named people or things; a fiction channel says so.

## 4. No reaction shots (general)
Seen in: channel #1, shots of a character reacting were where viewers left.
Fix: every picture shows the thing being said.

## 5. The payoff is the most surprising fact (general)
Fix: hold the most surprising figure or fact for the payoff, and make the last line loop into the first.

## 6. Every picture once, and things arrive with motion (owner, 2026-11-20)
Seen in: v-20261120-07 (Stanley: 6 photos across 17 cuts, the shop shelf 4 times) and v-20261120-08 (Nike: 3
sneaker photos 5 times); both had no overlay, no document and only plain hard cuts. Owner: "same images are
being used many times… effects are very less".
Fix: a Short shows each picture once (only the loop's last picture may repeat the first); at least 3 devices
beyond photos (document or headline always, then depth, montage, polaroids, listing, sink, split, timeline,
map, products…); an overlay on every other beat (arrow, stamp, date, label, lowerThird; never a red ring, owner 20 Nov); leave `enter`
out so every layer arrives with a whip, zoom, focus, drop or glitch. Code: `doc_video_problems`.

## 7. Narration is never slow (general)
Seen in: the owner's notes on How Tech Works (Shorts), Disaster Desk (long) and Rise & Fall: "increase the pace".
Fix: no channel's narrator speaks under about 164 words a minute in a Short or 189 in a long video (generator/pack.py MIN_SPEED enforces it).

## 8. Show what happens, not only the count (general)
Seen in: Disaster Desk's Hyatt long video, 22 Nov: "only showing death counts is not enough. Make that scenes."
Fix: when a line says what happened to people or things, the beat shows it happening in the place (the crowd under the walkway, the cars on the bridge), never gory (director.md).

## 9. A named place, company or person shows its real mark (general)
Seen in: Geography's Russia Short, 23 Nov: "Where is the flag of Russia??" (its area grew in plain red); earlier notes on the first finance channel's Shorts.
Fix: a country on the map shows its flag (the code check fails a map video whose named country never gets one), a company its logo, a person their photo head.

## 10. A long video's first sentence states the stakes (general)
Seen in: Disaster Desk's Hyatt long video, 22 Nov: the hook was "good only for people who want to see the full video".
Fix: the first sentence says the small cause and how big it got, in plain words (agents/studio.md); the title stays free of it.
