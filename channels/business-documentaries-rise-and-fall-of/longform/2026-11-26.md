## Craft findings
1. **The first recap comes too late.** Crocs: chapter cards at 0:52, 2:45, 4:55, 6:33, 8:37, 9:52, 11:59; the first
   "so let's take stock" at 8:40 (57%), the second at 11:51. Pizza Hut: first "so far" at 9:59 (59%). That's
   7.5-8.5 minutes with no recap or tease, against the spec's every 2-3 minutes. The chapter cards come every
   2-2.5 minutes, which is right, so the gap is the recap line, not the chapters.
2. **Cliffhangers into chapters are already there.** Both films put a question just before most cards ("And then
   what?", "Should it sell?", "But there's one country…"). Keep it.
3. **The ending is its own chapter.** Crocs spends 77 s on "what really happened", Pizza Hut 100 s with a
   "Chapter eight" card at 90%. A card that promises a lesson reads as the end, and resolved stories lose viewers.
   Worth a test, not a rule: we have no curve yet.
4. **The payoff lands on time**: the crash at 43% (Crocs), the confession at 37% (Pizza Hut), near the recipe's ~38%.
5. **We can't see dips yet**: `metrics/videos.json` has no retention curve for long videos.
