## Craft findings
1. **The intro is measured at 30 s, and it checks the title.** Our recipe tells the rise and fall by 50 s and the
   promise by ~65 s. That's fine for the promise, but whatever the title and thumbnail claim should be visible and
   said inside 30 s, or the Intro drop is YouTube's signal that the click felt baited.
2. **The earliest turn in our references is at 19 s** (KFC's "but at home…"), with the ranking chart by 26-36 s.
   Labubu tells the whole rise and fall in 60 s. A fall by 30 s is worth testing, not adopting: we have no data.
3. **The Crocs sample's opening already does most of it**: named at 0.3 s, the first figure at 4 s, the fall by
   about 11 s, the going-concern line, then the question and chapter card. Keep it as the model.
4. **We can't judge a long hook yet**: `metrics/videos.json` has no retention-at-30 s field.
