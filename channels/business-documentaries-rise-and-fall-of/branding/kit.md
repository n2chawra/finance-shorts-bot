# Branding kit: Business documentaries

Made 19 Nov 2026. Create the channel on YouTube with one of these (a Brand Account), then click
**Connect YouTube** on the Channels page.

## Name ideas (handles checked free on YouTube on 19 Nov 2026)

| Name | Handle | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Craze & Crash** (pick) | @CrazeAndCrash (also @CrazeCrash, @CrazeAndCrashDocs free) | says the channel's top kind in three words: the craze people bought into, then the crash; alliterative, easy to say, works for booms, backlash and falls |
| **Cult to Clearance** | @CultToClearance | the arc of a product boom and bust: a cult favourite that ends in the clearance bin |
| **Hype to Hollow** | @HypeToHollow (also @HypeToHollowDocs) | the hype, then what was left inside; good for rise-and-fall stories |

Clash search (YouTube channel search and the web): no channel, product or trademark filing under any of
the three exact names. "Craze & Crash" only meets off-niche car-crash gaming channels named "Crash
Craze" and one small "CRAZE CRASH"; trademark aggregators show only filings for the single word CRAZE
(board game, restaurants, textiles), none with "crash". "Hype to Hollow" sits near two small gaming
channels ("Hype Hollow", "Hollow Hype"). "Cult to Clearance" has no matches at all, but is the longest
to say. Dropped as taken or too close: Peak & Plunge, Boom & Bust (@TheBoomAndBust, @BoomAndBustFiles),
Boom Then Bust, Boomtown to Bust, Red Ink (@TheRedInk, @RedInkFiles, @RedInkDocs), Margin Notes,
Shelf Life, The Ledger Files, Marked Down, Hype & Ruin, Clearance Aisle, The Fizzle and Fizzled (many
channels of that name), Fad & Fall (too near the reference Fortune & Fall). Check the handle again when
you create the channel.

## Description (paste into the channel's About)

Beanie Babies were supposed to pay for college. A soda changed its recipe and lost its fans. A toy shop
everyone loved went under. We tell the full story of the things people bought, the brands they turned
on and the companies that rose and fell: the craze, the decision, the crash, and who won the business
war. Every story is built from the record (filings, sales figures, news reports and archive photos) and
told from around the world, not one country. Long documentaries twice a week, a short story most days.

Every fact and figure is sourced. Not financial advice.
