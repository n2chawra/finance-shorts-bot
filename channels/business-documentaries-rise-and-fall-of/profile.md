# Business documentaries

**What it is.** A long-first YouTube channel of business documentaries: how a product, a brand or a company
rose and fell, told from the record. Two 15–25 minute films a week and one 30–55 second Short a day, each
Short cut from a film's best minute where one exists. A hybrid documentary: licensed footage and archival
photos, the real document with a highlighter on the word, our own motion graphics; no cast, no presenter,
no AI scenes.

**Who it's for.** A global English-speaking audience led by the US, with large shares in the UK and
Europe (Germany, Poland, Sweden, the Netherlands), India, the Philippines and Southeast Asia, and Australia
(`research/comments.md`). Many are not native speakers; they know the brands and remember the fads.

**What it covers.** Product booms and busts (a thing people bought, the craze, the crash); brand backlash
(a recipe, size or price change that lost the fans); company rise and fall; business wars (X vs Y). Scandals
and finance appear inside these stories, never as the topic.

**What it never covers.** Stock tips or investment advice; accusations no court, regulator or filing
supports; other channels' clips, TV ads or news footage; graphic detail of deaths; AI scenes; news for its
own sake.

**Where facts come from.** SEC and other company filings, annual reports, court records and opinions,
regulator findings, press releases and reputable outlets, named on screen. Every figure is sourced or
computed by code, and the fact review checks each one.

**Rules from the research.**
- Settled stories only; every accusation attributed on screen to its source. Truth is the defence, "allegedly" is not.
- Every asset comes through `generator/media` and its licence log; no identifiable stock person on a negative beat; logos only to identify.
- Original script, our own graphics, real variety: never a narrated compilation.
- No real person's words in the narrator's voice: quotes appear as text.
- A beat on how the story went outside the US when it differs.
- Journalistic tone, so advertisers stay.

**Disclaimer.** Based on public records and reporting. Not financial or legal advice.
