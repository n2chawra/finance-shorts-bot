# Quick verdict: business documentaries (rise and fall of companies, corporate scandals)

**Call: go, with conditions.** Long business documentaries do very well on the niche channels, and small channels
break out. It is the best-paying niche we have looked at. It needs two build items before the samples: a licensed
footage fetcher and a documentary editor.

Scan of 19 Nov 2026 (`scan.json`): 6 viewer searches + 4 references, 4,744 videos from 45 channels. The searches also
caught news and Indian TV channels (CNN, NBC, WatchMojo, MX Player…), so the numbers below use the **22 on-niche
channels only**.

## Money (estimates, sources in the research notes)

- Long RPM for business and finance docs is about **$5–12**: OutlierKit's CPM of $10–25 gives roughly $5.50–14, and Stan reports $4–15. Shorts pay $0.05–0.20 (InfluencerMarketingHub and Digiday interviews), so Shorts are for reach.
- Partner programme today: 1,000 subs plus 4,000 watch hours. **From 1 Feb 2027 it doubles to 8,000 hours or 20M Shorts views** (YouTube's announcement). We will most likely join under the new bar, which a channel of 15–25 min long videos reaches fastest.
- Sponsors are common in the niche (NetSuite, Zapier, Surfshark, ShipStation).

## Can we make it

- **Look**: a hybrid documentary made from licensed stock footage, Commons and archive photos, EDGAR and court documents as props, and our flat-kit charts, timelines and maps. It renders in the cloud (no 3D).
- **Kit gaps (build items)**:
  1. A media fetcher for Pexels, Pixabay, Commons (video) and EDGAR that writes a licence log; a render fails on an asset that isn't in it.
  2. A documentary editor in Remotion that puts footage, slow-pan photos, graphics and documents on one timeline.
- **Voice**: a Kokoro American narrator. A generic narrator needs no AI label. A real person's quote is shown as text, never voiced.
- **Policy**:
  - Inauthentic content is judged on the whole channel, so every video needs an original script, our own graphics and real variety.
  - Advertisers pay full rates for journalistic coverage of misconduct.
  - Defamation: truth is the defence, so we tell settled stories (filings, regulator findings) and attribute each claim on screen.
  - Pexels bans showing an identifiable person in a bad light, so no stock faces on scandal beats.

## Conditions

1. Build the media fetcher and its licence log before the samples.
2. Build the documentary editor.
3. Settled stories only, every claim attributed on screen, no identifiable stock person on a scandal beat.
4. Original scripts and real variety (inauthentic content).
5. Long videos of 15–25 min, 2 a week; Shorts for reach.
6. No real person's words in the TTS voice.
