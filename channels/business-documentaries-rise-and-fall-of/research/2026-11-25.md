## Motion and pacing: what our numbers show
**Viewers leave where the picture slows down after the hook.** Our two Shorts with drop points both lose the most
viewers 1.7–2 s into beat 2:
- Stanley loses 19 points at 5.5 s.
- Nokia loses 37 points at 5.8 s.

In both Shorts the hook changes picture about every 1.3 s (3 layers in 3.8–3.9 s). Beat 2 then slows to one or two
layers over 4.5–5.6 s; Nokia's beat 2 is a single document held for 5.6 s. The second drops (Stanley −11 at 13.3 s,
Nokia −21 at 13.0 s) also come in beats with only 2 layers over 6–8.8 s. Across all 13 Shorts we show a new layer
every 1.9–3.0 s overall. The recipe asks for cuts every ~3–4 s, so the opening is fast and then it brakes.

The words matter too. Both beat 2s look back instead of moving on: Stanley says "Not a new cup… it barely sold",
and Nokia repeats the price. So the slower picture can't be separated from the words yet. That's why this goes in as
a **look challenger, not a lesson**: `doc-beat2-cut-rate` (the hook's picture rate held through 15 s, judged on held
after the hook). It is placed first among the look tests because it's the only one built on our own drop points.
The current test is a first-second one, so the next cycle is a look test and this would run then.
