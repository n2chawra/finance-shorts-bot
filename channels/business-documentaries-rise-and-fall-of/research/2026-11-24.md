## Voice and sound: what top Shorts do that we don't
**Our pace.** All eight Shorts are am_fenrir at speed 1.35 × tempo 1.2: 185–217 words a minute (median ~203).
That is the recipe's ~210, the owner's 1.5x choice of 19 Nov. Vendor benchmarks put documentary narration at
135–150 wpm and call 180+ tiring, but none of it is measured on Shorts, and I couldn't read a transcript of any
niche Short tonight (YouTube pages return no text). **No challenger**: the evidence is opinion, and the pace is the
owner's explicit call. If Stanley's 28% stayed holds as the other Shorts' data arrives, pace is one candidate worth
evidence next Tuesday.

**Our mix is on target.** Music sits 17.6–18.2 dB under the voice on all eight (target ~18), peak −1.0 dB, mean
−17.6 to −18.3 dB, LUFS −15.6 to −16.5. The pre-reveal drop (−16 dB, 0.6 s) and 16 effects a minute are in
`channel.json`. Nothing broken.

**Length, not sound, is the gap with the closest channel.** Rise Fall Repeat (19.9K subscribers) runs its Shorts
at a 101 s median across 148, and its four biggest are 107–147 s (Marvel 2.28M, Toyota 511K, "He Gave It Away for
FREE" 487K, YKK 314K). Ours run 33–48 s. That's already the `doc-short-length-50` challenger with this evidence; no
new entry.
