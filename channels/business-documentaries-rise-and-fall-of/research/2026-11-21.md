## Policy and algorithm (tonight's topic)
No November change was found. The two 2026 changes that matter, checked against how we ship:
1. **"Inauthentic content" is now "Generic or repetitive content"** (16 Jul 2026, a renaming and clarification, no new
   rule). Three things can't earn: generic or repetitive videos, unsatisfying or off-putting ones, and AI personas
   giving finance, health or legal advice. Our risk is the first: a narrated compilation of stock footage. The
   profile already forbids it, and the recipe's real documents with the highlighter, our own figures and a single
   sourced story per video are the original value YouTube asks for. We have no persona (no presenter, no cast).
2. **AI labels** (27 May 2026): one prominent label for photorealistic AI-made or altered content, which YouTube now
   applies itself if a creator doesn't disclose. Animation and light edits only get a note in the description.
   We use real licensed photos and footage and a synthetic narrator that never voices a real person's words, so
   `containsSyntheticMedia: false` (`workers/lib/youtube.ts`) stays correct. If the channel ever adds AI-made
   realistic scenes (the profile forbids them), that flag would have to change; nothing to do now.
Algorithm: nothing new and sourced this week; the Shorts feed still judges a video by whether people stay, which is
what the weekly loop measures.
