## Policy and algorithm (tonight's topic)
- **New since our last check (21 Nov): the YPP overhaul announced on 10 Aug 2026, starting 1 Feb 2027.**
  - New applicants will need 8,000 watch hours in 365 days or 20M Shorts views in 90 days. Today the bar is 1,000
    subscribers plus 4,000 hours or 10M Shorts views.
  - Members earn Shorts ad and subscription revenue only in a 90-day window with 10M+ Shorts views. Long videos keep
    earning either way.
  - Premium pools pay creators 55% for long videos and 45% for Shorts.
- For a long-first channel, this makes the long films the route to monetisation. A Short's job is to send viewers to a film,
  and that is already in the profile. It doesn't touch any agent's rules or our code, so it goes to the owner as an `info`
  notification.
- The "generic or repetitive content" rule (renamed in July) and the AI-label rule (May) are unchanged. Our directed shots,
  sourced facts and per-video code already meet them.
