## Kinds to avoid

- **Corporate scandal and fraud as the topic**: long 0.86×, 23,012 median; Shorts 2,170 median. Use the
  scandal inside a rise-and-fall instead. Also the legal risk is highest here.
- **Finance deals and private equity Shorts**: 0.59×, 0 hits in 51. (Long is "low" at 0.78× though the
  median is 238,214: Modern MBA's big audience; not ours to start with.)
- **How a business makes money**: 0.87× long, 0.81× Shorts; crowded (12–13 channels in 30 days).
- Off-niche the scan caught (news, TV, celebrities): 3,259 of 4,744 titles; ignored.

## How many to post

`counts.json`: **1 Short a day and 2 long videos a week** ("channels with a Shorts hit post about 1.1 a
day"; "7 small channels had a long hit (hit rate 11%), so 2 a week"). The owner's request said the same
(2 long a week, 1 Short a day). Uploads 2 a day of our 100.

## Shorts, long or both

Both, **long first**: long on-niche median 121,440 vs Shorts 18,210, and long RPM is the money (verdict).
Length: long **15–25 min** (the scan's best band: 158,096 median, 169 videos); Shorts **30–55 s** (under
35 s: 54,049 median; 70 s+: 10,846), with longer Shorts as a loop test.

## The look

A **hybrid documentary**, no cast and no presenter (`style.json`: cast false; footage is the largest
shot kind, then text cards, charts, photos). Target mix: stock footage 35–45%, archival photos 15–20%,
**documents and headlines 15–20%** (the signature: the page on screen, highlighter on the word),
motion graphics 20–25% (figures as objects that fall: price flip, split-flap, ghost bar). One backdrop
and one grade over every source; one accent colour, red for losses; chapter cards with a music drop.
What the references have that we don't: old TV ads and news footage (not licensable) → Commons and
public-domain photos with an archival grade, and our own motion graphics; article screenshots → our
own typeset cards quoting the source.

## The first second

The object on screen in frame 1 (or the document with the figure highlighted); the subject named by
1 s (`style.json` long: 0.5 s median); a figure before 10 s (7 s median); the whole rise and fall in the
first 60 s, then "it wasn't X, it was one decision", then the title or chapter card. Shorts: the
title and first line state the same paradox; the big figure by 5 s; a claim, not a question.

## What viewers ask for (comments, two videos)

Collectible bubbles (Beanie Babies ~12 mentions, Funko, Squishmallows, Pokemon cards vs Labubu), recipe
and price changes (Pepsi's sweetener switch ~20, shrinkflation ~7), dead sodas (~6), New Coke, the
Pepsi Challenge. Complaints to design against: **missing facts** and **US-only framing** (a global
audience) → research breadth and a "how it went in other countries" beat; errors spotted at timestamps
→ the fact review.

## What the verdict got wrong

- It led with **scandals**; the numbers say scandal-as-topic is weak (0.86×). The channel is booms,
  busts, backlash and wars, with scandals inside them.
- Its demand figures excluded off-niche channels by hand; the labelled families now back that: 1,485
  on-niche titles.
- The 4 references: MagnatesMedia had no uploads in 90 days and Modern MBA runs 38–46 min films; the
  small channels (Fortune & Fall, Bagholder Association) are the better model for us.
