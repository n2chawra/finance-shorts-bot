## Competitor teardown
**Rise Fall Repeat: the "long Shorts win" evidence was a time effect.** Overall its 100+ s Shorts have a median of
20,985 views against 1,406 for the ones under 35 s. Within the same age window, though, length makes no difference:
Shorts 120–200 days old got 1,323 (35 under 60 s) and 1,048 (10 at 60+ s). Only its last 90 days grew, and by then
every Short was 60+ s. I've corrected the `doc-short-length-50` challenger's evidence. Our build spec took its
"test longer Shorts" idea from this channel.

**Ravate** (English Shorts; Hindi ones left out): business Shorts (Nike, Toyota, Steve Jobs, Burger King), median
24K, six above 500K. Titles all start "Why…", "How…" or "The hidden…", plus an emoji. Matched by age, 60–100 s beat
under 60 s in 2 of 3 windows (42.6K vs 28.5K; 17.8K vs 9.6K; then 24.3K vs 28.0K). That weakly supports the 50–55 s
challenger, nothing more. Question titles got 23.0K against 24.3K for the rest, so no evidence for
`doc-short-question-hook` either way. Its flops were fraud and audit stories (EY, Paytm PAN cards, Byju's:
1,100–2,000), which fits the recipe's rule to avoid scandal as the topic.

**Bagholder Association: the title family is the finding.** Its 9 "X Is (Finally) Getting What They Deserve…" films
have a median of **464,541** views (Pepsi 1.39M, Doritos 1.28M, Krispy Kreme 734K, Gillette 653K, Wendy's 465K,
Burger King 102K). Its other 51 have a median of **28,440**, on the same kind of subject (KFC, Pizza Hut, LEGO,
Five Guys). All run 16–20 min, all about a food or consumer brand the viewer knows. Proposed as a long-video title
test (`long-title-verdict`, low confidence: one channel, and its best brands sit in that family). The test uses our
own words, never their phrase.

**Company Man** (1.8M): "X vs. Y" median 291,835 (4) against 160,302 for the whole channel. This supports the
long business-war share in the recipe. **Signal 404**: every Short is under 15 s with almost no narration, a visual
loop (the bent iPhone 11.8M). That's a different format from ours, so there's nothing to test from it.
