## Hooks: what top Shorts do that we don't
**Question titles vs claim titles, each channel against itself.** I took the 19 channels with 5+ question titles
and 5+ claim titles. The closest business-Shorts channels do *worse* with a question: Rise Fall Repeat's question
Shorts get 0.71× the median of its claim Shorts, Ravate 0.73×, Signal 404 0.77×, Unravelled by Shankar 0.92×. Tiny
documentary channels do better with questions: The Ledger Files 1.38×, Ascendants 1.63×, Tech & Titans 1.66×.
Across all 19 the median ratio is 1.09×. Over the last 30 days, 23% of question Shorts and 22% of claim Shorts beat
their channel 3×. **So the scan doesn't favour a question title; if anything the business channels nearest to us
favour the claim.** I added this to the `doc-short-question-hook` challenger's evidence. The champion (a paradox
claim) stays.

**Our own openings (views only, not a verdict).** The two Shorts that opened on a thing the viewer held, stated as a
paradox, both took off: "Stanley sold … a year. Then one cup went viral." (1,139) and "Tamagotchi could die in your
pocket, and kids still lined up to own one." (764 within hours). The three old enough to judge that opened on a deal
or a payment stalled at 12–46: the $35 Swoosh, "Nokia was in every pocket. So why did Microsoft pay billions…",
Dollar Shave Club's $4,500 video. That fits the object-first champion and the channel's "thing in your hand" rule.
I added it to `doc-document-first`'s evidence (that challenger tests opening on the document instead). Notice that
the Nokia line names an object too, but its question is about Microsoft's deal, not the phone. Six Shorts and no
retention data, so this is a pattern to watch, not a change.

**Signal 404's "Nokia 1998 to 2013 Rise and Fall"** got 2.7M views on 20.7K subscribers in a 14 s visual loop. Our
48 s Nokia Short got 46. Same subject, a different format (almost no narration). It doesn't fit our recipe, so
nothing to test (as on 22 Nov).

## Topic feed (route `video`, all global Shorts, priority normal: no reference has 5M+)
Before tonight the feed had 9 open Short topics, about 4–5 days of Shorts at 2 a day, so I added 3 even though they
score below the open ones (5.35, 5.35, 4.38 against 6.1–7.7). Their references are smaller breakouts, and their

1. `2026-11-23-toys-r-us-735-short` (`rise_fall_short`, retail): Chapter 11 in September 2017 with $5B in debt to
