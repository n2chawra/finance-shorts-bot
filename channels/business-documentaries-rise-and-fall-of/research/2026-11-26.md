## What top Shorts do that we don't (titles and the first frame)
- **Our titles without a figure hold viewers better than our titles with one.** The three Shorts titled without a money
  figure held 48.5%, 54.3% and 56.8%:
  - "Nokia Was in Every Pocket. Microsoft Bought It Anyway"
  - "Why Did Kids Love Tamagotchi, a Toy That Could Die?"
  - "Toyota vs Mercedes: The Car Built to Steal Its Buyers"

  The three led by a $ figure held 29.2%, 33.3% and 11.8% (Stanley, the Swoosh and Dollar Shave Club). With n=3 a
  side, and the topic mixed in (all three figure-led Shorts are founder or boom stories), this is a test, not a
  lesson. It goes in as `doc-title-no-figure` (first-second, judged on stayed to watch), after the open first-second
  candidates.
- The niche's breakout titles name a brand everyone knows and ask what happened to it ("What happened to Nutella?",
  "WTF Happened to Labubu?"). `doc-title-what-happened` is already on the list for long videos.
- YouTube's own title and thumbnail A/B test still doesn't work on Shorts (help page). There is nothing to build for
  Shorts. For long videos, Studio's notifications already ask the owner to use it.
- Best and worst by average viewed: Toyota vs Mercedes 78.3%, Nokia 56.7%, Swoosh 52.1%, against Stanley 43.1% and
  Dollar Shave Club 43.7%. The best two are a business war and a fall. The worst two are a boom and a founder story,
  told through their figure. This supports the title test above. No separate proposal.
