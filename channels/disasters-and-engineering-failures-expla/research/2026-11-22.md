**What they share:**
1. **The breakouts are this month's events.** Every Short above 1M in the niche is about a crash in the last 3-5 weeks (Florida Learjet on 1 Nov, Spain's trains on 28 Oct), told within days, with the clock and the data. Fault Lines' long videos show the same thing: Learjet 1.94M and 551K, New Orleans 118K, everything older 2-52K.
2. **The historic Shorts on our exact feed topics did poorly.** Major Disasters made Hyatt walkways, FIU, Oroville, Tacoma and Teton as 43-67 s 3D Shorts: 193-1,612 views each. Our 18 setup topics are mostly these kinds of story. A historic Short works when it has a big reference behind it: FedEx 80 at 7.6M on a 27K channel, the South Tower at 35K on 747.
3. **Titles name the figure against the clock.** "Jet Fell 27,000 Feet in 60 Seconds", "26,000 Feet in About a Minute", "The Tanker's Rudder Got Stuck. Two Minutes Later It Hit a Cruise Boat". This goes on the list as challenger `dd-title-figure-clock`. Our first title is a plain question.
4. **Longer Shorts.** World explained's breakouts run 107-115 s and Inside Decoded's 81-98 s. Major Disasters' 43-46 s Shorts have a 193 median. The setup scan said 35-50 s is best, so the evidence conflicts. This goes on the list as `dd-short-75s` (format kind), not into the recipe.
5. **A live instrument panel in every shot** (vertical speed, altitude). That's already challenger `dd-instrument-always`.

**Voice:** all four use a calm AI narrator at a normal pace (~140-160 wpm); World explained has a medium music bed. Our bm_lewis at the recipe's pace matches. Nothing to change.
