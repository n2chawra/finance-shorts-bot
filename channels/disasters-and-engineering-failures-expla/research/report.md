## Style targets (`style.json`, 3 Shorts + 4 long sheets)

- **Art style:** 3D animation in every measured Short. Long videos mix 3D reconstruction, flat diagrams, maps and report-style cards.
- **First second:** in 2 of 3 Shorts the subject is named by 2 s and a figure (altitude, date, speed) appears by 3 s. Long
  videos open at the moment of failure or on a silent date/warning card.
- **Long length:** median 651 s (608–695). The owner chose 8–10 min.
- **Cuts:** 2.5–5.6 a minute in long videos (from `sound.md`), 28 a minute in Shorts. The long seconds-per-shot figure
  comes from sampled sheets and is not reliable.
- **Voice:** about 134–164 words a minute, short sentences with a pause after each.

## Sound

Three of the four references are voice-only with hard silences. Fault Lines has a beatless bass drone about 33 dB under
the voice, rising 16 dB between sentences and dropping out before a chapter card. Long videos carry 3 effects a minute
or fewer. We win here with a designed bed, mechanical foley, and silence followed by a sub-boom at the failure.

## Comments' themes

- "Rules are written in blood": end on what changed.
- The turning figure (50 alive, then only 10 survived).
- Human moments.
- Viewers explaining the physics themselves: they want technical depth.
- Countdown clocks.
- Blame questions: stay with what the report says.
- Famous links.
- Follow-ups when the preliminary report lands.

## Gaps

- **Recent crash reconstructed fast:** the 1 Nov Learjet 60 crash pulled 8.3M, 3.1M, 2.8M, 1.9M and 917K views across
  channels within 3 weeks. It needs a fast lane and a follow-up.
- **Stunt / ride / amateur gone wrong** in long form: 1.93×, 409,809 median, posted by few channels.
- **Space mission failure:** core, a gap.
- **Flight-sim what-if Shorts:** core, a gap.
- No reference shows the failing part itself or the real report page. That's our edge.

## Risks turned into setup rules

1. **Flooded bottom** → quality gate: no video ships unless its first minute beats the references' hook (QA and the
   owner's sample gate).
2. **Repetition / inauthentic content** → look rotation (10 recipes, never one of the last 4) enforced in code; original scripts.
3. **Tragedy and ads** → no gore, no bodies, no victim close-ups, no "horror" framing; deaths stated plainly; end on what changed.
4. **Blame and defamation** → only what official reports say; recent events labelled preliminary.
5. **Realistic 3D** → "3D reconstruction · not actual footage" on screen, plus YouTube's synthetic-content disclosure on upload.
6. **Real voices** → cockpit and witness words shown as transcript text, never voiced with TTS.
7. **Cloud render time** → 3D only at the key 60–120 s, or those beats rendered on the PC.
8. **Licences** → US government reports and diagrams (public domain), Commons photos with credits, never other channels' footage.
