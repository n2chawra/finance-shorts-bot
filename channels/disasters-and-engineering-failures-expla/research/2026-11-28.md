## Policy and algorithm (tonight's topic)
- **No new Shorts policy in November.** The newest changes stand:
  - **Partner Program, 1 Feb 2027:** new applicants need 8,000 watch hours (now 4,000) or a higher Shorts-views bar (outlets disagree: 10M or 20M in 90 days). Existing partners unaffected. With long videos at 0-16 views, this channel's route is long-video watch hours; owner `info` notification written.
  - **1 Oct "Prioritizing original content on Shorts":** less reach for channels that mainly re-upload other creators' videos without significant changes. Voice-over over someone else's footage and template bulk edits don't count as original. Our promo Shorts are new scripts, new beat code and our own render, never cuts of others; self-cut promos aren't addressed. No change needed, but it backs the profile's "original scripts and a new look every video".
  - **AI labels (May 2026):** one label for photorealistic synthetic content, an overlay at the bottom left of a Short, auto-applied when undisclosed; YouTube says it changes neither reach nor monetisation. Our realistic 3D shots already carry "3D reconstruction · not actual footage" and the upload discloses; nothing to change. The bottom-left overlay sits inside the zone our captions already keep clear.
