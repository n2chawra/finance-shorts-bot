## What top Shorts do that we don't (pacing and motion)
| | Shot changes | First 10 s | Instrument on screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Athena_Explains, 7.4M | about 1/s in the hook, then 5–7 s 3D shots | about 10 cuts | none |
| Inside Decoded, 1.9M | 7–20 s held 3D shots after a 3 s title | 2 | telemetry panel (clock, altitude, rate) |
| World explained, 2.5M | 11–25 s shots | 2 | vertical-speed meter in every shot |
| **Ours** (4 Shorts) | **a size change about every 1.6 s, start to end** (27–33 in 46–49 s) | 6–7 | only in some beats |

The sheets are sampled, so their cut counts are lower bounds. The pattern is still clear. The references are fast at the hook and then slow down while they explain, and a live reading (altitude, rate of fall) carries the tension through the held shot. Ours runs at one even rhythm the whole way. Lac-Mégantic's 23% held after the hook fits that picture, though one Short proves nothing. That gives the challenger **`dd-hold-after-hook`** (kind look, area motion, judged on held after the hook): keep the fast hook, then hold each explanation shot for 4–6 s, with a slow push, motion inside the shot and one ticking instrument. It sits in `cycle/backlog.json` after `dd-pause-before-reveal`. The liveliness gate and the three-shot-size rule still apply.
