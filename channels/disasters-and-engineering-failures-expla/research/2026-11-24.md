## Titles the breakouts use (for the owner's note; Studio writes the long titles)
The niche's biggest breakouts say plainly **what happened, to what or whom**, and add one striking fact or "what really happened":
- "A 15-Year-Old That Crashed Plane Into Skyscraper" (15.7M, onca)
- "What actually happened during Kobe Bryant's final flight" (10.8M, 68x)
- "How Did the Florida Plane Crash Happen? Jet Fell 27,000 Feet in 60 Seconds" (2.56M, 669x)
- "The Bridge That Twisted Like Rubber" (1.1M, 77x)
- "What Really Happened on Fireball Ohio State Fair" (1.23M, 7.8x)
- "Korea's Death Mall" (8.2M, Rotten Mango)

None of them is a riddle about a part ("The Gauge Said Normal. The Tower Was Nearly Full."). A stranger needs to know the event before a detail can make them curious. Studio should use this shape for long titles: the event in plain words, then one striking fact or a how or why. Example: "How a Refinery Exploded While Its Gauge Said Everything Was Fine". Our Shorts titles already ask a plain how or why, so this isn't a Shorts challenger.
