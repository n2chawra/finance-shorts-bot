## Craft findings
- **The failure comes late.** In the references the moment of failure falls at 60% (Kolkata, 345 of 579 s), 60% (Learjet dive, 362 of 608 s) and 48% (Spain, 413 of 854 s) of the runtime. The hook states the outcome; the reconstruction is the reward for staying. Our recipe's chapter order already puts it at chapter 5 of 7, but nothing in it says when that should be in the video.
- **The middle is kept moving by coming back to one picture.** Fault Lines revisits one 3D flight-path ribbon from new angles (0–30 s, 160 s, 350 s), so the viewer always knows where they are. Kolkata uses one flat side elevation in which the exact part moves as it is named.
- **After the failure comes a general-principle chapter, labelled as such.** Learjet does how a spiral dive works and then a storm cross-section, each tagged "general principle". This keeps people through the investigation section.
- **We drop the recap.** Only 1 of our 3 long videos has a recap-and-tease beat, though `agents/scout.md` asks for one every 2–3 minutes.
- **Low CTR, tiny sample.** Hyatt's CTR is 0.8% on 126 impressions. That is too few to act on; thumbnails are next Thursday's focus.
