## Description (paste into the channel's About)

A train passes a red signal. A walkway is hung from the wrong rod. A ferry leaves port with its doors open.
Disaster Desk tells the true story of disasters and engineering failures, one at a time: the minutes
when it went wrong, the chain of small decisions that led there, the physics of how it failed, and the
rule that changed afterwards. Every story is built from the official investigation (NTSB, AAIB, RAIB,
TSB, CSB, NASA boards and public inquiries), with 3D reconstructions, engineering diagrams and the
report itself on screen. Calm, careful and respectful of the people involved.

New long investigations every few days, with a short from each one.

## Avatar (draw on our kit, don't render yet)

Flat 2D, ink-outlined, works at 98 px. A dark navy circle with a single **cracked steel I-beam** in
cream, seen end-on, so its "I" shape reads as a letter. One thin **red hairline crack** runs through the
beam's web: red always means the failure, as in our looks. A thin mono ring around the edge, like an
instrument bezel, with one red tick at the top. No text, no flames, no skull: it should look calm and
technical, not like horror.

## Banner (2560×1440, safe area 1546×423 in the centre)

A wide **engineer's cross-section** in our Blueprint look: a navy ground with a faint grid. Left to right
in one continuous line drawing: an airliner's profile, a train on a bridge truss, a dam's cross-section
and a ferry hull, each in cream line-work. On each one, **one part is drawn in red**: the trim tab, the
signal, the spillway slab and the bow door. Centre (safe area): "DISASTER DESK" in our mono label face,
with the line "What went wrong, and the rule that changed" underneath. Bottom right, small mono tag:
"From the official reports". Optional 3D variant: the same four heroes as clay-material models on a
desk, lit low from one side, for when the 3D hero library exists.
