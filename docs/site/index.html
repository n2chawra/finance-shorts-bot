Finance Shorts Uploader

A personal tool, not a public service.

Finance Shorts Uploader is a private automation used by one person to publish short, animated personal-finance explainer videos to their own YouTube channel. It creates the videos, uploads at most one video a day to that channel using the YouTube Data API, and reads that channel's own YouTube Analytics numbers to see how the videos perform.

There are no other users and no sign-ups. It does not access anyone else's YouTube account or data.

The videos are illustrative only and are not financial advice.
