## Craft findings (charts)
- **We show two numbers and leave the gap to the viewer.** Rich vs poor and early vs late are stories about a gap. Our chart draws two glowing lines, and the gap has no figure or shading. Fixing this takes a `gap` fill with a chip and a `compare` maths kind for the figure.
- **No counter at the tip of the line.** The guide lists the left-to-right "wipe" and the "counter" as core chart motion. We have the wipe and a pulsing dot, but no number on the dot.
- **Two focal colours.** We draw red and green at full glow every time. The guide's first rule is one strong colour for the focus and grey for context.
- **No zoom-out reveal.** The compounding "it looks flat… now zoom out" moment is a natural re-hook mid-video. Our axis is fixed for the whole beat.
- **Chart lines narrate the picture instead of the takeaway.** In L-20261019-02 the chart beats begin "Here is the whole journey…" and "Look how the red line…". The guide says the title or line should state the conclusion.
- **`how_they_make_money` needs a fee figure.** Both new topics need "a fee as a share of spending" (the swipe fee). No maths kind computes it.
