## Craft findings (the first 30 seconds)
- The three beats are: prove the title with a concrete example (0–10 s), say why it matters to the viewer (10–20 s), then a one-line roadmap (20–30 s). Never open with a greeting, a backstory or a full table of contents. One creator reports first-30-second retention going from 52% to 78% after cutting a 45-second intro to 3 seconds. That is an anecdote, but the direction is consistent across every source.
- L-20261018-01 gets the first two beats right: a bank SMS in frame one, then the interest stake in beat 2. Its fourth beat ends on "Let's find out why", which promises nothing specific. That line should be the roadmap.
- Mid-video, the advice is to open a loop every 2–3 minutes by teasing the next part, close the main question only in the last fifth, and put the best moment at about two thirds of the runtime rather than at the end.
- A visual change every 5–10 s is already enforced by our liveliness gate, so there is nothing new there.
