## Craft findings (thumbnails and titles)
- **The text on the thumbnail matters more than the face.** In the 507K study, 4-6 words was the biggest gap of any feature: 30.6% of breakouts against 26.5% of underperformers. Faces, smiles, brightness, contrast and colour made no meaningful difference. Drawn graphics had a small edge (41.8% vs 38.1%). A faceless, drawn channel loses nothing by being one.
- **Longer, specific titles broke out more often.** Breakout rate by title length: under 30 characters 26.1%, 45-59 33.9%, 60-74 34.8%. Our long titles have been 38-55 characters. This conflicts on length with the open "under ~55 characters" item.
- **Our thumbnail references are often off-topic.** Studio copies thumb-refs, but the savings topic's references were kids' rich-vs-broke skits (45M), the billion topic's was MrBeast's house video, and the gym topic's was a home-gym list.
- **We can't measure any of this yet.** Impressions and CTR are null for all 64 of our videos, so no thumbnail or title has ever been judged on clicks.
