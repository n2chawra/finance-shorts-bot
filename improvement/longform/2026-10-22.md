## Craft findings (thumbnails and titles)
- **Our three Test & Compare thumbnails are one picture three times.** L-20261021-01 and both earlier long videos use layout `story` with the same hero, receipt, arrow, background and character position, and only the words change. YouTube says this kind of test runs long and ends inconclusive.
- **The `versus` and `secret` thumbnail layouts exist in code, but no README tells the writer.** That is why every long video since 2026-10-19 used `story` three times.
- **Thumbnail words repeat the title.** The title is "…which one are you?" and the thumbnail says "Which money level?". The words should add a stake or a feeling the title doesn't already give.
- **We test one title.** Test & Compare now tests titles too.
- **Our titles are long and newsy.** The outliers are 31–39 characters: a named thing plus one loaded word ("The 'Car Loan' Trap, Explained"). Ours run 57–63 characters, with figures and a news framing.
- **A new long shape:** "The ___ trap, explained", a deep dive into one trap. It has 6.04M, 2.72M, 1.23M and 1.06M views. formats.json only has `trap_alert` for Shorts.
- **Minor:** in the `story` layout, the cast member sits in the bottom-right corner, under YouTube's duration badge.
