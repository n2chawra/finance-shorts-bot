## Craft findings (retention shape)
- **We answer the question too early.** L-20261019-02 settles "minimum vs fixed payment" at 72–76% of the runtime. The last 24% is a source card, a checklist and a recap, which is the "pivot that feels like an ending" every guide names as a late dip. YouTube's own advice is the other half: put the strongest moments earlier and keep one reason to stay.
- **Chapter cards are silent pauses.** Six cards sit at 11/29/39/62/84/93%. A card with no spoken stake is the slow stretch the guides blame for dips. One worked example lost 35% of viewers over a 45 s stretch with no narration. Chapters help people skip forward, so they reduce exits overall, but each card needs a spoken re-hook with a figure right after it.
- **A spike can mean confusion.** Our densest "how it's computed" beats put a percentage and a floor into one sentence over one picture. Split them.
- **We can't measure any of this yet.** The stats worker already stores each upload's retention curve, so per-chapter hold is a small addition. It stays empty until the long videos reach 100 views.
