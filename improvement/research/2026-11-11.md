## The owner's title note
The owner is right. The last 7 titles hide the famous name: "the biggest channel on earth" instead of MrBeast, "the basketball star" instead of Magic Johnson, "the airline" instead of Delta.

The cause is the variant system:
- On 6 Nov it promoted `title: plain` and dropped `figure_or_name`: plain n=4, median 460 views, against n=24, median 140.
- The comparison is confounded by length. Among 20-27 s Shorts, figure/name titles have a median of 908 (n=5), and there is no 20-27 s plain Short with data yet.
- `config/arms.json` and the variant system are owner-only, so this went to the owner as the notification `title-arm-hides-famous-names`, with exact steps.

## Learn from our numbers
- **Best % viewed:** v-20261023-01 (165%), v-20261021-05 (72%), v-20261105-06 (70%). All 25-28 s, spoken at 172-234 wpm, with an extreme close-up on the answer (the first two in the older engine).
- **Worst:** v-20261020-03 (7%), v-20261016-02 (13%), v-20261019-02 (18%). 36-60 s news or SIP Shorts.
- **The one change:** the pace of speech. Hence `shorts-voice-back-to-october-pace`.

1. **`shorts-voice-back-to-october-pace`** (pacing, experiment, high)
   - Voice speed × tempo 1.4 on Shorts for the "on" arm, 80-100 words in the 20-27 s band.
   - Medium confidence, S effort. Metric: views48h.
2. **`actions-that-change-things`** (motion, experiment, raised to high with tonight's counts)
   - At most 25% highlight actions.
   - Medium confidence, M effort. Metric: avgViewPct.
3. **`extreme-close-on-the-turn`** (motion, experiment, normal)
   - One extreme close-up on the answer at the turn or payoff.
   - Low confidence, S effort. Metric: avgViewPct.
