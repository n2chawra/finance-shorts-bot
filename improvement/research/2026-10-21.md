What our last 6 Shorts do:
- **Hook beats are long.** First beat: 2.9, 3.9, 4.9, 5.4, **8.6**, 3.0 s. On v-20261020-02 (ECB), the contact sheet shows the same picture at 0 s and 5 s. → `hook-beat-under-4s` (experiment, after the running hook experiment ends).
- **Long beats go quiet in the middle.** 8 beats per Short, 49 in all; 11 run over 6 s (longest 9.1 s), and **18 of 49 have a stretch over 3 s with no named event**. That breaks our own rule ("a new picture every 3–5 s"). The liveliness gate still passes (median 1.25–2.30, still share ≤5%) because idle motion moves pixels; it doesn't show new information. → `event-gap-max-3s` (adopt: it enforces an existing rule).
- **Empty frames.** On v-20261020-02, the headline beat at 10 s shows a title and "since mid-September" on an empty background (the 2.5% arrives later), and the split (30 s) and kinetic (40 s) scenes leave two thirds of the frame empty. Top Shorts this week are character-led throughout. → `no-empty-lower-half` (experiment).
