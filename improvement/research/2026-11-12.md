## What top Shorts and thumbnails do that we don't (tonight's topic)
- **Long-video thumbnail (the owner's note).** L-20261111-01's thumbnail breaks five things the hits do:
  1. **Its words contradict the title.** The thumbnail says "MILLION? CUTE." and the title says "$1 billion". A thumbnail should add the stakes to the title, not argue with it.
  2. **There are about 10 things in it:** a wallet, an hourglass, a fountain, a crowd, a rocket, a banknote, two pallets, a warehouse and Sharma. At phone feed size (about 168 px wide) the bottom row is noise. The hits in `thumbnails.json` use one subject, a face and 2–3 words.
