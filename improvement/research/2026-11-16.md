## Learn from our numbers
- **Best % viewed since 1 Nov** (50+ views): California tax 89.1%, Bezos 85.0%, fortune split 84.7%. **Worst:** "A cash pile this big..." 49.2%, MrBeast 41.6%, Buffett/Coca-Cola 41.1% (61 s).

That is lesson 1 (never a smaller figure after the big one). The reviewer's lessons already cover it, so no new proposal.
