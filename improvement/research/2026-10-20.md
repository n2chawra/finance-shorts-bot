**Voiceover (the topic of the day).** I couldn't hear any competitor: no transcripts were reachable. What our own files show:
- **The narrator sounds like a cast member.** `NARRATOR_VOICES` contains am_fenrir and af_bella, which are also Sharma's and Sikka's voices. On v-20261018-04 and L-20261018-01 the narrator was am_fenrir *and* Sharma spoke, so one voice played two people. → `narrator-not-cast-voice` (adopt).
- **Pace:** 169–205 spoken wpm (136 on v-20261018-03), 122–180 wpm over the whole video. That is around the top of the 150–180 wpm the guides suggest, at the intended tempo 1.3 (~185). No change without data.
- **No pauses before reveals:** the voice fills 86–90% of every Short, with ≤ 0.5 s between lines, and the payoff number is spoken straight on. → `reveal-beat-pause` (experiment, low confidence).
- **Voice quality:** by Kokoro's own grades, af_heart (A) and af_bella (A-) are the best English voices. am_michael, am_fenrir and am_puck are C+, and hf_alpha (Priya) is C. The best British voice is bf_emma (B-), above every male voice we use. am_michael narrated 5 of the last 9 Shorts. Choosing voices is done by ear, so it goes to the owner (notification), not to an experiment.
- **Levels:** peak −1.0 dB and mean −15.9 to −17.6 dB on every Short, close to the −18 dB target and below YouTube's loudness normalisation. Music ducks by 60% under speech (the guides say 6–12 dB; 60% ≈ 8 dB). Nothing to change.
