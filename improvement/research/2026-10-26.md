## Doable changes, ranked
1. `no-exposition-before-8s` (hook, **experiment**, high): beat 2 is a reaction or question ≤ 2.5 s; no beat over 6 s before the first reveal. Effect high × confidence medium ÷ effort S. Measure: avgViewPct.
2. `midpoint-rehook` (script, **experiment**, normal): one ≤ 6-word line at 40-60% that reopens the question, with a punch-in and a hit. Effect medium × confidence low ÷ effort S. Measure: avgViewPct.
