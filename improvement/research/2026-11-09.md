## Doable changes, ranked
1. **`big-numbers-in-words`** (text, **adopt**, high).
   - Write ≥1M amounts short-scale in titles, descriptions and on screen.
   - check-plan rejects runs of 7+ digits.
   - It is an owner-reported fix. High confidence, S effort. Metric: views48h.
2. **`running-counter-rehook`** (hook, **experiment**). On scale stories, one counter stays on screen from the hook to the payoff and changes every beat. Medium confidence, M effort. Metric: avgViewPct.
3. **`powers-maths-kind`** (template). A repeated-multiplication calculation. It unlocks the pyramid-scheme (1.89M), penny-doubling and viral-spread stories, with the world population as a World Bank fact. Medium confidence, M effort. Metric: views48h.
