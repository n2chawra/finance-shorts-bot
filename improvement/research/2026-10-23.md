## Not doable
- Minecraft/Futurama-style videos: a game's or show's look is someone else's copyrighted asset. Our own cast and sets already give the same "a world you know" effect.
- Reddit-story, call-in-show and family-drama formats (Reddit Gigglez, Caleb Hammer, Reelshub): they need real people's stories or recorded voices. We tell illustrative cases with our cast.
- Trading tips, IPO and single-stock videos (Jio IPO, IDBI, AMD, SoFi): outside `channel/profile.md`.
- A gold price or GST rate in the Dhanteras video without an official page: left to the fact reviewer, or kept off screen.
