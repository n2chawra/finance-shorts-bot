## Doable changes, ranked
1. `loudness-in-render-json` (sound, **adopt**, normal). Record integrated LUFS and the bed's level under the voice in `render.json` and `metrics/videos.json`, and warn outside −16 to −13 LUFS or a bed less than 18 dB under the voice. Effect: lets the evaluator judge the 2 Nov mix. Confidence high ÷ effort S. Metric: qa_failures.
2. `asker-voice-contrast` (sound, **experiment**, normal). On question-chain Shorts the asker and the explainer get voices of different gender, using existing voices only. Effect small × confidence low ÷ effort S. Metric: avgViewPct.

## Not doable
- **A slower bed (~76 BPM like The Curious Plot):** the variety rule keeps tempo at 80–120 BPM, and only the owner changes `generator/variety.py`.
- **Always cinematic:** genre rotation is a variety rule.
- **A recorded, cloned or online voice for a "real" narrator sound:** hard rule. Kokoro only.
- **Mastering louder than −14 LUFS:** YouTube turns it down, so it gains nothing.
