## Policy and algorithm (tonight's topic)
- **1 Oct 2026, Shorts originality ranking.** TeamYouTube: Shorts will "further prioritize original content and reduce the reach of content re-uploaded from other creators". The target is channels "primarily aggregating or re-uploading other creators' videos". Rene Ritchie's advice: add "your own voice, storytelling, or distinct point of view"; narration over someone else's clip or template-based bulk edits don't count. **For us:** every Short is our own story, drawn and voiced by us, so this ranking change doesn't target us. No YouTube official text names animation or AI voices.
- **The spam policy's "automated or synthetic mass-production"** allows "a few variations" of a video but not tools used "to flood our platform with repetitive content" (example: AI scripts and images reused across many videos). Inauthentic-content enforcement for monetization is reported to be channel-level. **For us:** the variety rules, the directed per-video beats and the true, sourced facts are the right defence. One gap: every Short's description says "An animated story with illustrative numbers", the same boilerplate on every upload, and since 12 Nov it is untrue (the numbers are sourced facts). That's tonight's `adopt` proposal.
- **Monetization 1 Feb 2027** (10M Shorts views in 90 days for Shorts revenue) is unchanged; it's already in the owner notifications.
- **AI labels:** reported not to affect reach or monetization. Our videos use no realistic synthetic people, so no label change is needed.
- No confirmed November 2026 algorithm change; third-party "Shorts algorithm 2026" posts disagree with each other and are not used.

For a video with `facts`, the description says "Sources: …" instead of "An animated story with illustrative numbers"; the hard-rule disclaimer stays. ## Not doable
- Changing the narrator to a human or cloned voice for "originality": breaks the offline-voice hard rule, and YouTube's text doesn't penalise synthetic narration of original stories.
- Cutting to fewer Shorts a day to look less "mass-produced": the upload count is the owner's setting; no evidence ties our cadence to reach.
