## What top Shorts do that we don't
Hook evidence tonight is guidance, not data: no source publishes first-second swipe rates (the "70% decide in 2 s" figure traces to a TikTok report). What the sources agree on: name the viewer's question or the answer in the first line, make the first frame show the same promise, cut warm-ups and greetings, use real stakes, and judge each Short against your own similar Shorts (viewed vs swiped away, then the retention curve).
