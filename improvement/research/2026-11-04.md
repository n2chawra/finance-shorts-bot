## Doable changes, ranked
1. `items-enter-on-their-word` (motion, **adopt**, high). Items start hidden and enter on the word that names them, with a craft gate for any item visible more than 0.3 s before its word. Large effect × high confidence ÷ M. Metric: avgViewPct.
2. `company-wordmark-not-fake-icon` (motion, **adopt**, high). Coded name tiles for real companies and no pun props. Copies no logos. Metric: avgViewPct.
3. `source-once-per-source` (text, **adopt**, normal). "Data: …" shows the first time each source is used, and the hard rule is still met. Metric: qa_failures.
4. `person-intro-tag` (script, **adopt**, normal). One sourced clause under 8 words that sets the person's scale, in the promise beat, not the hook. Metric: avgViewPct.
5. `actions-that-change-things` (motion, **experiment**, normal). glow, shake and stamp at most 25% of actions; the rest transform. Low confidence ÷ M. Metric: avgViewPct.
