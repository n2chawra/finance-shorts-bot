## What top Shorts do that we don't
- Sources agree the first frame's on-screen text, the title and the description are the main discovery signals. Ours: titles are descriptive questions/statements, fine, but two of seven manifests have **no description or hashtags at all** (v-20261014-01 and -03, the chai/subscription ones), so they lose those signals.
- Indian finance channels that perform lean on **specific schemes/fees named in the hook** (Sukanya Samriddhi, Jan Dhan, hidden bank charges). Our titles are generic ("Which subscription...") with the product name only sometimes present.
- Custom thumbnails are now allowed on Shorts; advice is to keep key elements out of the bottom fifth. We already generate `thumbnail.png`; unverified whether the publisher sets it.
- Hashtags: 3-5, relevant; `#Shorts` is optional now. Ours use 4 with `#Shorts` first, which is fine.

## Doable changes, ranked
1. **Always write description + 3-5 hashtags** (generator validation). High confidence, S. Metric: qa_failures (missing metadata), views48h.
2. **Name the specific product/scheme in the title's first ~40 characters** (e.g. "PPF", "SIP", "FD") and put the headline number in the title when the template allows (number computed by code). Medium, S. Metric: views48h by title style.
3. **Title length target 40-70 chars with the keyword up front**; our titles are 45-60 already, so mostly a guard. Low, S.
4. **Verify the publisher sets the custom thumbnail and that the thumbnail has <=4 words, no text in the bottom fifth.** Medium, M (publish worker is owner territory; propose only).

## Not doable
- Anything that downloads competitor assets or music: breaks the synthesised-audio rule.
- Tag-stuffing titles/hashtags: sources say it lowers CTR and risks the inauthentic-content flag.
