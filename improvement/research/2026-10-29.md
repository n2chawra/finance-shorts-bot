The winners run 60-115 s. Most titles are a question with a famous subject: Why / How / What / Is ___ really. The hashtag-stuffed "power of money" edits stay under 160K.
- **Titles (topic of the day).** Metricool's 2026 study says 61% of Shorts views come from the feed and 9% from search. The title is a second hook in the feed, and it matters most in search and on the channel page. The vendor advice (keyword in the first 40 characters, under 60 characters) is unproven. Our own numbers say more. Among the 15 Shorts since the reset:
  - The 4 titles that open Why/How/What have a median of **1,167 views**, against 331 for the rest.
  - The 7 that name billions or the rich have a median of 1,103, against 452.
  - The one title with a cast name ("Rohan gets paid $5,000. Watch where it goes") has 45 views on day one. A stranger doesn't know who Rohan is.

  → `title-asks-the-viewers-question` (**experiment**, normal, low confidence: our question titles are also our billionaire topics).
- **Early read on the running `title-names-the-moment` experiment:** its 'on' titles (who + an everyday moment) have a median of **119 views** over 5 Shorts, against **1,103** for 'off' over 5. The experiment's metric is avgViewPct and the videos are different ages, so the evaluator decides. I added this to the proposal's evidence for the improver. The new proposal waits until this experiment ends, so only one title test runs at a time.
- **Descriptions and hashtags:** ours open with the hook as a question, then the assumptions, the advisor line and the disclaimer, plus 4-5 hashtags with `#Shorts` first. I found nothing new this week that supports a change. Keep it as it is.
- **Covers / first frame:** both covers I looked at are good: 2-4 huge words with one word picked out in yellow, a character and the money object, and a lit set. YouTube's custom Shorts thumbnails (rolled out on 25 Jul 2026) can only be uploaded on desktop, reach Partner Program channels first and have no A/B testing. Every channel can still pick a frame on mobile. Covers only show on the channel tab, the home page and in search. → owner `info` note, no pipeline change.
