## What top Shorts do that we don't
- **Top of the market (25-31 Oct):** "Rich Mindset V's Poor Mindset" 2.21M in 4 days (60 s). "Want to Become a Billionaire? Follow SB MD MK!" 1.02M (48 s). "Why Money Won't Make You Happy" 499K (24 s). "Who Was The 1st Billionaire In These Industries?!" 441K (72 s). "HOW TO MAKE YOUR FIRST MILLION" (Diary of a CEO) 326K (101 s). "How Much Money Exists in the Entire World?" 70K in 16 hours. Titles are short questions or "X vs Y" about billionaires and the rich, as last week. Length 40-100 s, so our 45-70 s setting sits inside the winners' range.
- **Hooks (topic of the day).** I couldn't reach the competitors' audio, so this comes from their titles, the hook research and our own data. Most guides agree on three things. The swipe decision on Shorts comes at about 2 s. The hook has to work on mute. The strongest openings make a counterintuitive claim or open a curiosity gap that the first 3 s don't close. All the numbers are vendor data: directional only.
  - Our own data says the same, more strongly. Our 'secret' hooks are claims the viewer can't resolve ("Billionaires never sell their stock"). They keep 156% past the hook and 75% viewed. Our 'question' hooks ask and half-answer in one breath, for 38% viewed. "It's not the burgers. So how does McDonald's really make money?" gives away half the twist (not burgers) and then asks.
  - The owner's own fix turns it around: put the belief to the viewer ("you think…?") and deny it. That makes the viewer commit to a guess in the first second.

  → `hook-you-think-x-youre-wrong` (**experiment**, high).
- **Animation (the owner's channel note).** The 31 Oct contact sheet shows the problem plainly. From 5 to 45 s every still is a wide shot: a restaurant or a character in the middle, with money as small tags ($20, $3, $4,000,000) floating beside them. Nothing travels from one thing to another, even though the whole story is money moving (customer → franchise owner → McDonald's). The lit night sets appear only at the turn and the payoff.
  - The money-scale Shorts that do travel in the market show money as a physical object that moves (stack counting 65-72K in 17-21 s; our own "$1 billion in $100 bills" got 1,105 views).
  - No vendor or creator page I found measured camera moves against retention, so this is our own call from the stills and the owner's notes.

  → `money-travels-not-pops` (**experiment**, high). It sits with the open build-beat proposals `build-beats-on-lit-sets` and `first-action-by-1s`, and all three could share one flag.
- **Re-hooks:** nothing new beyond `midpoint-rehook` (still open).

**Voiceover (every night).** No competitor audio or transcript reachable again. The money-scale Short in the market still credits a separate narrator voice ("Voice Credit"). No new voice proposal. `cast-voices-the-story` and the listen-test note for the owner stay open.

**Music (from the owner's note).** v-20261031-02 used cinematic in E at 94 BPM. `_cinematic` in `renderer/engine/music.py` has no steady pulse: two-bar string swells, a drone, toms on beats 1 and 2.5, and a progression that starts on a minor chord. Cinematic is our best genre by the numbers (13.4×, n=6), so the change keeps the genre and adds energy. → `cinematic-bed-gets-a-pulse` (**experiment**, normal, low confidence).

## Doable changes, ranked
1. `hook-you-think-x-youre-wrong` (hook, **experiment**, high). The hook puts the viewer's belief to them as a question and denies it, never giving the answer. Start it after hook-voice-matches-frame ends. If the improver can run only one, it replaces claim-then-wait-what. Effect high × confidence medium ÷ effort S. Measure: avgViewPct (and hookHold).
2. `money-travels-not-pops` (motion, **experiment**, high). Money travels on a path between the props it connects, with the camera following. Props enter from off-frame or grow out of the previous prop instead of popping and shaking. Effect high × confidence medium ÷ effort M. Measure: avgViewPct.
3. `cinematic-bed-gets-a-pulse` (music, **experiment**, normal). Adds a soft kick and hats from bar 1, starts on a major chord and lifts at the turn. Effect medium × confidence low ÷ effort S. Measure: avgViewPct.
