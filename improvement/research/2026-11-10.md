## What top Shorts do that we don't (music, sound, voice)
- **I could not hear any competitor tonight.** YouTube served no player data or captions to a plain fetch, so there are no words-per-minute or mix estimates from other channels. The web has no real study either. The vendor guides (FluxNote, GoFaceless) repeat 160–180 wpm and music at 15–20% of the voice level, with no data behind it. Our ~175 wpm (tempo 1.2) is already inside that band.
- **So our own numbers are the evidence, and they are consistent for three weeks:**
  - Acoustic and synthwave are our two weakest genres. Pop and cinematic are the strongest.
  - `music-follows-story` (27 Oct) has been waiting. I re-raised it as high priority with the new numbers and changed its pick for money-machine stories from synthwave to pop.
  - Genre is chosen by `generator/variety.py` (owner-only) and the variant system (owner-only). So a "music" option in the variant system goes to the owner (notification `variant-option-music`).
- **Voice:** the narrators that hold viewers are af_bella, am_michael and af_heart. am_fenrir works as a hook voice (3.27×) but not as the narrator (0.61×, n=4). New experiment: `narrator-from-the-strong-voices`. Kokoro has no new voices in 2026, still 54 since v1.0.
- **The hook (the owner's note):** all three Shorts of 9 Nov open "<belief>? **Watch this.**" The writer prompt already asks for "You think X? It doesn't." (adopted 2 Nov), but the runs ignored it, and three identical endings in one day is also a repetition risk. New adopt: `hook-denial-not-watch-this`, a check in code, not a prompt.
