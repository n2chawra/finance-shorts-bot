## What top Shorts do that we don't
- **Top 30 (26 Oct):** billionaires still. AniMatters' animated billionaire story **2.79M** (135 s, 19,300 views an hour), the casino Short 1.79M (73 s), "Why Billionaire is Billionaire" 773K (96 s), "What If Billionaires Divided Their Money Among Everyone?" **204K** (89 s, new), "I Tried to Spend $1 TRILLION in One Day" 83K (39 s, narrator-credited), "Jeff Bezos's Security Costs A Whole Life" 36K, "It Would Take You 10,000 Years To Save A Billion" 32K (21 s). The winners stay 60-135 s; the 6-30 s "power of money" edits stay under 140K.
- **Pacing (topic of the day).** The blogs agree only on a visual change every 1.5-3 s and no cuts under ~1.2 s; none gives an animation-specific number. Competitor video pages still can't be read (footer only), so tonight's evidence is our own timelines.
- **Finding 1: the levels play on flat sets.** In v-20261027-03 the hook (0 s), the turn (40 s) and the payoff (48 s) stand in lit night sets with depth (skyline, lamp, light pools). Every level in between (stills at 5, 15, 20, 25, 35 s) is a flat lavender sky over a grey floor, one character and a prop, the top half empty except the counter. -01 and -02 are the same. The 30 s where the levels climb look less made than the 8 s around them. → `build-beats-on-lit-sets` (**experiment**, high).
- **Finding 2: one skeleton, to the beat.** The three new Shorts have 11-12 beats each; transitions cut 1 / match 5-6 / whip 4-5 / zoom-through 1 in all three; the turn framed close→wide, the payoff wide, the loop medium→wide in all three; and the last 9.6-10.5 s (turn + payoff + loop) carry **no prop actions** (only the speaker and captions move). Three videos in a row with the same shot pattern is the "template" look the owner and YouTube's inauthentic-content rule warn about. → `ending-beats-act-and-vary` (**experiment**, high: seeded camera/transition choice for the coded beats, actions on the words in the turn and payoff).
- **Finding 3: late first actions.** Several build beats fire their first action 1.3-2.2 s in ('case' 2.0, 'pallet' 2.2, 'invest' 1.6, 'decade' 1.4), so with the transition the picture holds still for ~2-3 s. → `first-action-by-1s` (**experiment**, normal, low confidence).
- Good news: build beats now carry 3-7 actions each (the old 33 s skeleton in v-20261026-02 had none), and shot sizes mix medium/wide/close/extreme in every video.

**Voiceover (every night).** Competitor audio still unreachable.
