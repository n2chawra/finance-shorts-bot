## What top Shorts do that we don't
- **Policy, the part that touches us.** YouTube's official monetization page (the "inauthentic content" policy, renamed July 2025, clarified on Creator Insider July 2026) does not demonetize AI use as such, but lists two things we must keep clear of: (1) "content that looks like it's made with a template… giving the impression of mass production", with "minimal variation across videos"; it allows series where "each video has a distinct storyline" (our variety rules and shot system already target this); and (2) content that presents "itself as a human expert providing advice" on finances, example "AI-generated podcast hosts offering financial guidance". Our narrator is a synthetic voice, so every line must stay a story about characters, never an adviser speaking to the viewer. The fact reviewer already rejects instructions; credentials or expert claims aren't named anywhere → `no-expert-persona` (**adopt**, required by the platform).
- **AI labels:** YouTube's disclosure rule covers *photorealistic* AI content; our cartoon cast doesn't need the label, and labels don't affect reach. No change.
- **Shorts pay changes 1 Feb 2027** (official blog, 10 Aug 2026): new YPP applicants need 8,000 watch hours/365 days or 20M Shorts views/90 days; Shorts revenue sharing only above 10M Shorts views/90 days; long videos keep earning below it. Owner info notification `ypp-2027-shorts-threshold`.
- **Algorithm:** the one well-documented change is still Shorts view counting (31 Mar 2025): every start *and replay* counts as a view; "engaged views" count for YPP. Vendor blogs disagree on whether swipe-away or watch time leads in 2026; nothing official. Replays counting supports a seamless loop.
- **Our endings don't loop in picture.** v-20261022-01 holds one static close-up of Rohan for its last 2.6 s (four identical stills, 32.8–35.4 s) and then jumps to a wide street; v-20261023-01 ends on a close-up of Sharma in a different set from its vault opening. The loop line already leads back in sound → `loop-picture-matches-hook` (**experiment**, ending).
- **Smaller notes (no proposal):** v-20261023-01 at 15.0 s shows a mid-transition frame with "AWAKE" half cut at the top and a ghosted stack (a travelling transition caught mid-way; fine in motion). v-20261023-02's single-word captions ("IT.", "FIRST.") are the word-by-word captions working as designed.
